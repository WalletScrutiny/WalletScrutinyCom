**⚠️ Warning:** The provider appears to be out of business. Its domain no longer exists.

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still defunct. cryptoring.io no longer exists in DNS. Its [last archived copy, from February 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210209235841/https://cryptoring.io/), shows a shop by CNICK selling NFC rings from 69.99 to 120 USD. The rings were advertised for "Sharing Cryptocurrency address", access cards and business cards, not for holding keys or signing transactions. We found no later trace of the product, so this stays a stub.

The [provider's website](https://cryptoring.io) is inaccessible.
We believe this hardware wallet was likely never produced and is now considered defunct.
