**⚠️ Warning:** Bitkee appears to be out of business. Its domain no longer resolves.

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still defunct. bitkee.com no longer resolves. Its [last archived copy, from November 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241107224825/https://bitkee.com/), still only says Bitkee is "shutting down production of metal cards" and asks readers to "check back in 2021". [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/bitkee/__fSOALgRumgqnsRH86IiLL45hKrq6WSYPcPn1UdyiX8k) lists the company as deadpooled. Bitkee sold laser-engraved metal key backups, not a signing device, so we keep this a stub.

On the [bitkee site](https://bitkee.com) they state the production of this wallet has been discontinued.
