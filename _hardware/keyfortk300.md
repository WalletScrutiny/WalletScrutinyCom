**⚠️ Warning:** KeyFort appears to be out of business. Its domain expired in 2021.

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still defunct. The Wayback Machine shows keyfort.io as an expired domain in [June 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210620041014/https://keyfort.io/), a domain for sale in [2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240325053805/https://keyfort.io/) and a gambling site in [October 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251008040500/https://keyfort.io/). Today the domain does not resolve at all. The device itself is documented in its [FCC filing](https://fccid.io/2AVA7-K300X) by KEYFORT PTE., but we found no shop or firmware updates, so it stays a stub.

This hardware wallet looks to be defunct, the provider’s [main site is not accessible](https://www.keyfort.io).
