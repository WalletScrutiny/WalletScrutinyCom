**⚠️ Warning:** Pi Wallet appears to be out of business. Its domain has changed hands.

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still defunct. An [archived shop page from January 2016](https://web.archive.org/web/20160112043332/http://pi-wallet.com/) sold the Pi Wallet for 124.95 EUR; from May 2017 the site returned errors. The domain had [expired by December 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241226232400/http://pi-wallet.com/) and showed an [unrelated Chinese-language website in June 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260607040859/http://pi-wallet.com/). Today it serves only an error page. We keep this a stub.

The [provider's website](https://www.pi-wallet.com) is inaccessible and we considered it defunct.
