## App Description

Safello is a regulated Swedish cryptocurrency brokerage and custodial wallet service that allows users to buy, sell, and store more than 20 cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, using payment methods such as Swish, Apple Pay, cards, and SEPA-style transfers. According to its Custody Policy, Safello acts as a custodial wallet provider, meaning the private keys are held and controlled by Safello rather than by the user. 

## Analysis

The company states that customer crypto-assets are segregated from Safello’s own funds, maintained in separate blockchain addresses, and safeguarded through Fireblocks’ MPC-based custody infrastructure. No source code repository is provided for the Android app, and Safello’s service operates entirely under a custodial model supervised by the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority.

> 2. Custody and safekeeping of customers’ crypto-assets
>
> In the course of the Safello Wallet service, Safello acts as a custodian wallet provider, where the private keys to the wallet are held by, and hence controlled by, Safello. The crypto-assets held in Safello Wallet are assets held by Safello for the customers’ benefit on a custodial basis.
