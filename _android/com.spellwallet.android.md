## App Description

Spell Wallet claims to be a multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet that uses MPC (multi-party computation) instead of seed phrases and allows users to create and access a wallet using an email or biometric authentication. 

It describes its functions as supporting the storing, sending, receiving, swapping, and staking assets including BTC, ETH, SOL, and TON, with integrated rewards, staking features, and a built-in Web3 browser.

## Analysis

Despite its claims, our [tests](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1999817551191404982) reveal that it **does not actually support Bitcoin**. We searched both 'By coin' and 'By network' and was not able to find evidence to support their claim.
