## App Description

Avici claims to be a mobile neobank-style app that aims to combine wallet, exchange, and payment card functionality under a single interface. 

There is an associated token based off the Solana blockchain.

## Analysis

Some users report difficulties completing key onboarding steps such as receiving account verification emails, and there are online discussions and third-party warnings about scam sites impersonating Avici or related token airdrops which have circulated on social media and crypto forums. 

We tested the app, but did not receive the email verification. There are no indications that this app supports Bitcoin. 

They recently held an ICO, where its [price peaked on Nov. 25, 2025](https://www.coinbase.com/price/avici) and subsequently crashed. Multiple similar domains are listed on its Google Play store. Some of these domains are no longer accessible. 

With that said, we can say that there is **no credible proof that this app supports Bitcoin**.
