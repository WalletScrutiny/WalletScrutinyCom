## App Description

WOTA is listed on Google Play as a portfolio tracking app for monitoring and analysing project and digital-asset performance. The App Store and website terms describe it more directly as a cryptocurrency investment app connected to WOTA accounts.

## Analysis

The current Play Store listing describes WOTA as a portfolio tracking solution, but the official terms describe the mobile app differently:

> The WOTA mobile application is a centralized finance (CeFi) wallet designed to help investors store their Ethereum (ETH) and Bitcoin (BTC) securely while earning strong monthly returns.

The terms also say users must open a WOTA account to access the services, deposit BTC or ETH into the wallet, and select an investment program. This makes the custody model account-based and provider-managed.

This app may support Bitcoin deposits, but it is a centralized finance account service, so it is **not verifiable as a self-custodial Bitcoin wallet**.

*As additional context, the linked website currently shows a browser certificate warning, and the social links listed in the iPhone app description appear to be unavailable - or made unavailable: Facebook content is inaccessible, Telegram reports no user, and the X account is no longer available. A [FastBull/BrokersView warning page](https://www.fastbull.com/brokersview/brokers/wota) labels WOTA as a scam, but this is only a weak secondary source, and we could not independently confirm its claimed Securities Commission Malaysia listing.*
