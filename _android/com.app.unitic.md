## App Description

Uniticexchange is a cryptocurrency trading application that, according to the developer, provides a platform for buying, selling, and trading digital assets and fiat pairs through a mobile app. The developer states that the service supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, Unitic Token, Shiba Inu, and more than 120 cryptocurrencies, with additional support for fiat transactions involving INR. Uniticexchange is presented as a regulated and KYC-compliant exchange, offering features such as spot trading, crypto deposits and withdrawals, and advanced trading options including signals and futures. The developer positions the app as a Web3-focused trading platform aimed at facilitating compliant and accessible participation in the crypto market.

## Analysis

According to their terms:

> 2. The Online Platforms provide access to a Digital Assets exchange for: (i) online trading of your Digital Assets by the use of fiat currency or any Digital Asset, (ii) enabling transaction of Funds which include deposit and/or withdrawals, and (iii) online storage and custody of your Digital Assets.

This is a **custodial** service.
