## App Description

Binance TH is a licensed digital-asset exchange operating in Thailand under Gulf Binance, offering users the ability to buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies through a fully KYC-verified account system. The app supports THB deposits and withdrawals via QR-based bank transfers and provides access to spot markets for BTC, ETH, and hundreds of listed assets. Trading features—such as charting, alerts, and portfolio tracking—are all executed through Gulf Binance’s centralized exchange infrastructure.
