## App Description

Niza Global is a cryptocurrency exchange and banking application that provides users with account-based wallets, trading services, and integrated fiat banking features such as IBAN accounts.

## Testing and Analysis 

Bitcoin and other assets are held in provider-managed wallets within user accounts rather than through on-device private key control.

According to the [FAQ](https://help.niza.io/hc/en-us/categories/22116589504657-FAQ) and [Terms](https://niza.io/en/terms), user funds are safeguarded by Niza Global under regulatory obligations, with transactions executed by the provider and subject to KYC, account limits, and possible suspension or freezing.

The application does not publish source code, does not claim self-custody, and operates as a regulated **custodial** platform rather than a standalone Bitcoin wallet.
