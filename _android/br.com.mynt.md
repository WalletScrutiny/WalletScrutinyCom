## App Description

Mynt is a Brazilian cryptocurrency investment platform that supports Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies, allowing users to buy, sell, and transfer assets starting from small amounts. User funds are held and managed on Mynt’s centralized infrastructure, with crypto balances consolidated within the platform. 

## Analysis

Mynt clearly positions itself as a **custodial** intermediary for digital assets:

The [Terms](https://www.mynt.com.br/assets/documents/Mynt%20-%20Termos.pdf) state that the platform 

“atuará no agenciamento, intermediação e corretagem de criptoativos” 
(it will act as agent, intermediary, and broker of crypto assets).
