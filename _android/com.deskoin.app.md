## App Description

From the Google Play listing:

> The French reference platform for buying, selling and trading among more than 115 cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Ripple, Aave, Bittensor, Kaspa, Pepe and Sui in complete security.

Deskoin is a French crypto exchange founded in Metz in 2018, registered with France's Financial Markets Authority (AMF) under PSAN number E2021-017, and registered with the ACPR (Bank of France) for custody of digital assets on behalf of third parties.

## Analysis

Deskoin is a custodial exchange. The AMF registration explicitly covers *"custody of digital assets on behalf of third parties"* — confirming the platform holds user funds. Users buy and sell crypto via euro bank transfers, SEPA, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. No seed phrases or private key access are provided.

No source code is available.

## Verdict

Deskoin is a **custodial cryptocurrency exchange**. Users do not control private keys.
