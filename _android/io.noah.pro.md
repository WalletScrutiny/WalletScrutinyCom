## App Description

BitradeX claims to be an AI-driven cryptocurrency asset management platform that automatically manages and optimizes user portfolios, supporting assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin through a single mobile interface. The app claims to offer no-KYC onboarding, automated custody, credit card and bank transfer funding, and round-the-clock customer support to help users participate in global crypto markets.

## Analysis

BitradeX’s User Agreement describes a custodial, account-based model where user “balances” are recorded as off-chain entitlements on an internal ledger rather than held in segregated on-chain wallets per user. The agreement explicitly states that user digital assets are not stored on-chain in separate wallets, and that BitradeX will not use specifically identifiable coins when executing instructions, which is consistent with pooled custody rather than self-custody.

> “These digital assets are not stored on-chain in separate wallets from digital assets held by other users or digital assets maintained by BitradeX for business purposes. An internal ledger is used to record the quantity and type of digital assets to which customers are entitled. Each user will have an off-chain ledger-based account or sub-account with a unique identifier (UID)…”

— [User Agreement](https://www.bitradex.com/en/user/user_agreement), Clause 19.12 “Digital Assets”

This service is **custodial**.
