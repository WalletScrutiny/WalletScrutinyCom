## App Description

Bitcoin.com.au is an Australian custodial exchange app that allows users to buy and sell Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies. It operates under Digital Currency Exchange registration DCE-100461150-001 with AUSTRAC, ensuring compliance with Australian financial regulations. Users transact through custodial accounts managed by the platform rather than through self-custody wallets.

## Analysis

[Section 7.2 of their terms says](https://bitcoin.com.au/help/terms-and-conditions/):

> We will hold all private keys for any Cryptocurrency we hold for you. We hold a majority of Cryptocurrency in secure offline cold storage protected in underground vaults.

This app is **custodial**.
