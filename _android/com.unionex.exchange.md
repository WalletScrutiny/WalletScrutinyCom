## App Description

UnionEx is listed as a cryptocurrency trading app for buying, selling, and managing digital assets including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Tether. The iPhone counterpart describes UnionEx as a trading app with access to perpetual futures and tokenized real-world assets.

## Analysis

The Play Store listing describes UnionEx as:

> a next-generation crypto trading app built for both beginners and professional traders.

No public documentation was found showing that users receive seed phrases, private keys, or full independent control over Bitcoin funds. This app supports Bitcoin trading, but it appears to be a **custodial** exchange account, so it is not verifiable as a self-custodial Bitcoin wallet.

The website also says:

> Asset Security
> We strictly protect your assets with advanced security technology, multi-factor authentication, and cold wallet storage.

This proves the keys are held by the provider.
