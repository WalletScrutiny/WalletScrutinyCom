## App Description

OrangeX is a cryptocurrency exchange app that enables users to buy, sell, and trade popular digital assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major cryptocurrencies.

The platform offers multiple trading modes, step-by-step onboarding, and features such as copy trading for users who want to follow professional traders’ positions.

OrangeX emphasizes security controls and account protection measures while providing 24/7 multilingual customer support.
