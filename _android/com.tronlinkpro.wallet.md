## App Analysis

[Testing images on x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2014519411974328563).

TronLink Pro is a self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet designed to manage assets on the TRON blockchain and interact with TRON-based decentralized applications.

The app **does not support Bitcoin** on the native Bitcoin blockchain, and any “BTC” shown in the interface refers to tokenized or wrapped representations on other networks.

It allows users to create or import wallets, manage TRX and TRC-10/TRC-20/TRC-721 tokens, stake assets, and sign transactions locally on their device.

TronLink Pro also integrates a built-in DApp browser and multi-chain features aimed at enabling access to DeFi, NFTs, and smart contracts within the TRON ecosystem.
