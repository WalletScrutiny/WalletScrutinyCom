## App Description

HashKey Exchange is a licensed virtual asset exchange in Hong Kong operating under Type 1 (Dealing in securities) and Type 7 (Providing automated trading services) licenses from the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). The platform enables users to deposit HKD or USD via bank account transfers from 16 countries and trade cryptocurrency pairs including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT, MATIC, AVAX, UNI, LINK, and other digital assets. The application provides fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-crypto trading services with deposit and withdrawal functionality for both fiat currencies and cryptocurrencies.

## Analysis

The application operates as a centralized cryptocurrency exchange where users create accounts and deposit funds for trading. The description explicitly states **"Your funds are held in separate accounts independent of our operations"**, confirming the platform maintains custody of user assets in segregated accounts. The deposit and withdrawal processes, bank account integration, and account-based trading model demonstrate a traditional exchange architecture where the platform controls user funds.

From the website:

> 90% of assets are stored in cold wallets with military-grade protection, backed by top-tier security partners.

No mention of private key management, seed phrase backup, or user-controlled wallets appears in the application description.

As a licensed and regulated virtual asset exchange in Hong Kong, HashKey Exchange functions as a **custodial** service where users trust the platform to hold and manage their cryptocurrency and fiat currency deposits.
