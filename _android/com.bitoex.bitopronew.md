## App Description

BitoPro is the official mobile application of the BitoEx/BitoGroup cryptocurrency exchange based in Taiwan, providing users access to a centralized trading platform The app enables users to deposit, trade, and withdraw cryptocurrencies through BitoPro’s exchange backend.
