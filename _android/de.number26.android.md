## App Description from Google Play

N26 Crypto is an integrated cryptocurrency trading feature within the N26 banking app, operated in partnership with Bitpanda Asset Management GmbH. It provides custodial crypto trading and storage, where Bitpanda executes transactions and securely holds users’ digital assets in cold storage.

## Analysis

Quoting from one of their [documents](https://n26.com/en-es/blog/how-to-buy-cryptocurrency-with-n26):

> N26 Bank does not offer or provide virtual assets to fiat currency exchange services or custody of virtual assets by itself. N26 Bank makes a functionality available in its app that technically allows the reception and transmission of purchase and sale orders of virtual assets executed exclusively by Bitpanda GmbH (FN423018k, at Stella-Klein-Löw-Weg 17, 1020 Vienna, Austria), a virtual asset service (VASP), and custody provider registered with the Austrian Financial Market Authority (FMA) and with Banco de España (N0023062C).

This may be done via API, where the custodian is Bitpanda. Nevertheless, this is still a **custodial** service.
