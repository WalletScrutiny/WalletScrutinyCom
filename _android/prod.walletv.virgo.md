## App Description

Wallet V is a mobile cryptocurrency wallet that integrates decentralized finance (DeFi) and Web3 services within a single application. It enables users to connect to Hyperliquid to trade perpetual futures contracts and perform cross-chain token swaps across Solana and multiple EVM-compatible networks such as Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon, Tron, and X Layer. The app provides in-wallet access to Polymarket prediction markets and tokenized stock products (XStocks) deployed on-chain. Additional features include a points-based rewards system (VPoints), a token launch platform (VDrop Launchpad on HyperEVM), and third-party security audits, with availability subject to regional restrictions.
