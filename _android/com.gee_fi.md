## App Description

GeeFi claims to be a non-custodial multi-chain wallet supporting 14+ networks including
Bitcoin, Ethereum, and others. Their website states "Your Keys, Your Crypto" and emphasizes
user ownership of private keys.

## Analysis

We tested the app and posted the results on [x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2059551417954897961). **No source code** repository was found — no [GitHub](https://github.com/search?q=%22com.gee_fi%22&type=code), GitLab, or any public code. Without
source code, the non-custodial claim cannot be verified. Investigation stops here.
