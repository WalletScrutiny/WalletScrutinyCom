## App Description

Dtunes is a Nigeria-focused mobile app that enables users to sell cryptocurrencies and gift cards and convert them directly into local currency.

The app supports trading assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT, alongside popular gift cards from brands like Amazon, iTunes, and Steam.

Dtunes provides a built-in crypto wallet, real-time price notifications, and instant cashout to a Naira wallet or linked Nigerian bank account.

It positions itself as a fast, secure, and user-friendly platform for converting digital assets and gift cards into cash within the Nigerian market.

## Testing and Analysis

We [tested](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2014614160647299564) the app, and notice the same pattern we see in a lot of Nigeria focused apps - the interface may seem like there is a wallet, but the features are limited to receiving and **not actually sending to an external wallet**.
