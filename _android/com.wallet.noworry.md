## App Description

Nowory claims to be an India-focused cryptocurrency trading platform that allows users to buy, sell, and withdraw crypto assets such as Bitcoin and Ethereum with zero trading fees through a mobile app. The app claims to support INR funding, mandatory KYC, and features such as instant trading, installment-based crypto purchases (“Nowory11”), discounted coin deals, rewards, and airdrops, with users receiving full crypto access from day one even when paying in installments. Nowory also claims to use custodial security infrastructure with encryption, multi-factor authentication, and continuous monitoring to protect user assets and trading activity.

## Analysis

From its Google Play description itself:

> Your assets are protected with SOC 2 Type II-certified custodians and industry-standard encryption.

This is a **custodial** service.
