## App Description

CoinSavi is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange operated by SaviLabs PTE Ltd., providing account-based spot trading for over 350 supported assets. The platform offers fiat deposit and withdrawal functionality, real-time trading tools, and high-liquidity order execution through partnered liquidity providers. All custody, security controls, and account protections—including the claimed 23 security layers—are managed directly by the exchange through its infrastructure. 

## Analysis

> 7.9 Effect of Suspension or Termination: we shall continue to maintain custody of all Digital Assets, funds, and information from such accounts, which may be turned over to any government or regulatory authority in the event that Your Account(s) was suspended or terminated pursuant to fraud investigations, investigations of violation of law, investigations of violation of these Terms, or court orders in any applicable jurisdiction(s).

The service requires user registration and operates entirely as a **custodial** trading platform rather than a self-custodial wallet.
