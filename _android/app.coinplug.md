## App Description

CoinPlug is an Android application for **crypto-to-fiat exchange in Nigeria**.

> "CoinPlug allows users to easily withdraw to their local bank account. The payout request is processed in less than 30 minutes including weekends."
> — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.coinplug)

Deposits are handled via custodial addresses:

> "Select your desired cryptocurrency and generate your wallet address. Deposit using the provided details."
> — [CoinPlug FAQ](https://coinplug.ng/media/posts/faq)

Withdrawals are only to **Nigerian bank accounts**, with **no mention of sending BTC to external wallets or apps**.

> "…provide a reliable platform to sell your cryptocurrencies instantly to your bank account."
> — [CoinPlug FAQ](https://coinplug.ng/media/posts/faq)

* * *

## Analysis


1.  **Is it a wallet?**
    Partially - it is an OTC custodial exchange app.

2.  **Is it for bitcoins?**
    Yes, BTC is listed among supported assets.

3.  **Can it send and receive bitcoins?**
    We tested the app and found no proof of sending; only crypto deposits and bank withdrawals are documented.

4.  **Is the product self-custodial?**
    No, it is custodial; deposits are credited to accounts they control.

## Verdict

CoinPlug provides custodial deposit addresses and enables crypto-to-fiat withdrawals only to **Nigerian bank accounts**. There is **no evidence or documentation** (we also tested and found a verification wall that disallows further features) that users can send BTC to external wallets. While it can receive Bitcoin deposits, its custodial, account-based model without external send functionality justifies the verdict of **nosendreceive**.
