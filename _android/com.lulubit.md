## App Description

Lulubit is a cryptocurrency platform operating in Guatemala, Panama, and Costa Rica that provides buy/sell services for Bitcoin, USDT, XRP, USDC, MATIC, and over 20 other cryptocurrencies with direct bank integration for fiat deposits and withdrawals. 

The platform offers yield-bearing accounts claiming "up to 7% annually" on deposited cryptocurrencies, a Lulucard Mastercard for spending crypto at merchants worldwide, and a virtual U.S. bank account service for receiving payments from PayPal, Payoneer, and Wise. 

Users can make free bank transfers using regional payment systems (ACH for Panama/Costa Rica, SINPE), send and receive crypto from exchanges like Binance and BingX, and convert between fiat and cryptocurrencies with no trading or maintenance fees. 

The service describes itself as "the bridge between your bank and your crypto," positioning as a regional cryptocurrency gateway with banking integration and card payment functionality.
