## App Description and Analysis

Solflare is a self-custodial mobile wallet designed for managing assets on the Solana blockchain, including SOL, SPL tokens, NFTs, and staking.

The app allows users to create or import wallets, stake SOL, swap tokens, and interact with Solana-based decentralized applications directly from the interface.

Solflare **does not support the native Bitcoin network**, and [any Bitcoin-related assets shown in the app are Solana-based token representations](https://www.solflare.com/prices/bitcoin/5Hx6RkCUHsJ5FeeCQQYhC3rJw1eNTFqTu6wtuzzZXbLj/how-to-buy/) rather than real BTC.

It positions itself as a secure and feature-complete gateway for users participating in the Solana ecosystem and its DeFi and Web3 applications.
