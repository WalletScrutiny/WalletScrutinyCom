## App Description

ByteWallet is a Bitcoin wallet app that lets users buy Bitcoin after linking a bank account and then send it to other people using Lightning Network payments. The app presents a fixed 1.99% total fee for purchases and states there are no minimum balances or monthly fees. It is positioned as a simple on-ramp and spending wallet focused on buying and using Bitcoin rather than trading. Instructions provide a way to generate a 12-word seed phrase.
