## App Description

Deffio is a multi-chain wallet that claims support for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, and token standards such as ERC-20, TRC-10, and TRC-20, allowing users to manage assets across more than 10 networks.

It provides seed-phrase–based wallet creation, indicating that private keys are generated and stored locally on the device.

The app includes real-time portfolio tracking and basic transfer capabilities across the supported networks, based on the provider’s public description.
