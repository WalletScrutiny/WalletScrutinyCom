## App Description

Iris Wallet (testnet) is an experimental Android application for testing RGB protocol assets. It allows issuance, sending, and receiving of RGB tokens, anchored on the Bitcoin testnet rather than mainnet. The app is intended only for experimentation with low-value assets and does not function as a mainnet Bitcoin wallet.

## Analysis

**Experimental nature**

“This is an experimental application and it is recommended to be used only with low amounts of bitcoin and low value assets.”
— Google Play

**Explicit testnet-only scope**

“ALL USE OF THE APP IS AT YOUR OWN RISK. YOU SHOULD NOT USE THE APP TO SEND OR RECEIVE TEST NET BTC OR RGB TEST NET TOKENS THAT YOU ARE NOT PREPARED TO LOSE ENTIRELY.”
— Iris Wallet Terms of Service

**Self-custody confirmed**

“You are solely in control of and responsible for storing and securing your private key for the App. Bitfinex Labs does not store a backup of, and will not be able to recover, your private key.”
— Iris Wallet Terms of Service

**Verdict**

While Iris Wallet (testnet) demonstrates RGB protocol functionality, it does not support mainnet Bitcoin. All operations are limited to testnet BTC and RGB test assets, making it **unsuitable for real Bitcoin use**.
