## App Description

Fearless Wallet is a self-custodial, open-source mobile wallet designed for Polkadot, Kusama, Ethereum, and other Substrate/EVM-based networks, enabling users to create or import wallets and fully control their private keys.

## Analysis

**The app does not support Bitcoin**, as it is built exclusively for account-based smart-contract and Substrate ecosystems rather than Bitcoin’s UTXO model.

Fearless Wallet focuses on staking, governance, and parachain crowdloans, positioning it as a DeFi and ecosystem-native wallet rather than a Bitcoin wallet.
