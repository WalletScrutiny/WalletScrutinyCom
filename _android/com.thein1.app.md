## App Description

IN1 claims to be an all-in-one crypto wallet, banking, and exchange app that allows users to hold, send, receive, trade, and convert cryptocurrencies alongside USD and EUR fiat accounts after completing KYC. It supports assets including BTC, ETH, USDT, SOL, and LTC, and offers integrated cards with Google Pay, crypto-to-fiat exchanges, and in-app trading and transfers.

## Analysis

Their security practices gives us clues regarding the custody of the assets:

> Security and transparency
>
> IN1 applies encryption, biometric login, and two-factor authentication to secure every transaction. Your funds are protected with a mix of hot and cold wallets. Fees and exchange rates are shown before confirmation — no hidden costs. IN1 delivers bank-level protection for your crypto, fiat, and card operations.

The usage of cold storage, implies that key management is in the hands of the provider, making this product a **custodial** service.
