## App Description

*Claimed:*

This app provides access to a centralized cryptocurrency exchange requiring user accounts. The app enables users to buy, sell, and trade various cryptocurrencies—including BTC, ETH, SOL, and other listed assets—through services operated by the exchange. It also offers features such as spot trading, and futures/derivatives trading.

> Your security is our top priority. We employ robust security measures to ensure that your digital assets are protected.

## Analysis

The registration sign up country choices is limited to India and Indonesia. Although the Play description indicates BTC trading, we are not able to verify this since we were not able to sign up. This is also not readily seen in the homepage - unlike other exchanges that show BTC-to-fiat charts even if the user has not yet signed up. 

The evident trades that we see [listed](https://cheezeebit.com/c2cList) are mostly USDT-INR. 

In its "User Protocol" page: 

> Unless otherwise permitted by the Platform, you may only sell such amount of Digital Assets as does not exceed the total amount of Digital Assets held in your Account and recorded in the Platform ledger, plus the applicable Transaction Fee (as defined below). Any attempt by you to sell more Digital Assets than the Platform records show exists in your Account after deduction of the applicable Transaction Fee will result in an unsuccessful trade and may be grounds for termination of the Account. 

Its latest news was dated Oct. 2024, 

> Cheezeebit supports spot cryptocurrency market viewing, and the market chart features advanced charting tools provided by TradingView

But the link points to a tradingview USD-BTC chart. 

From what we've observed, this is a **custodial** app.
