## App Description

Azasend is a crypto-fiat exchange app that allows users to buy, sell, and convert Bitcoin (BTC), USDT, ETH, and other assets into supported fiat currencies such as NGN, ZAR, and VND. The app supports direct bank deposits and withdrawals, meaning user funds are held and transferred through Azasend’s managed fiat and crypto wallets. It features automated withdrawals, integrated fiat wallets, and a platform token called AZA for transaction fees and rewards.

## Analysis

We were able to [find a BTC wallet](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1988541405896089895) but transacting on the OMNI network. The seed phrases were not provided leading us to conclude that this is a custodial service. The following statement quoted from their site further bolsters that point: 

> We carry the sole authority to refuse to accept any user or to complete any command to transmit, receive, send, withdraw, or convert money held in your Azasend Account at any time.

This is a **custodial** app.
