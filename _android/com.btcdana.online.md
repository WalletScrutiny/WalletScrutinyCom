## App Description

BtcDana is a closed-source CFD (Contract for Difference) trading platform integrated with MetaTrader 5 that offers [over 300 CFD instruments including cryptocurrencies](https://www.btcdana.com/) like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin.

## Analysis

According to [ForexDana.com](https://forexdana.com/) (the parent platform), users can trade "big cap cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, and more" through CFD contracts. The platform's [official website](https://www.btcdana.com/) shows cryptocurrency trading pairs including ALGOUSD, BONKUSDT, AAVEUSD, and ETCUSD, confirming active crypto CFD offerings.

However, the platform explicitly states that users trade derivative contracts rather than owning actual assets: "You don't own, or have, any interest in the underlying assets." This means users cannot send Bitcoin to external wallets or receive Bitcoin from blockchain addresses - all positions exist only as CFD contracts within the platform's centralized infrastructure.

While users can buy, sell, and hold "Bitcoin" positions for speculative purposes, **they cannot send or receive actual BTC on the blockchain.** This product allows Bitcoin price exposure without Bitcoin custody or on-chain transaction capabilities.
