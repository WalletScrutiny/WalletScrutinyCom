## App Description

BtcDana is a closed-source financial trading app that operates as a multi-asset CFD platform integrated with MetaTrader 5 rather than a cryptocurrency wallet.

The official website provides no verifiable evidence of Bitcoin trading, Bitcoin storage, or any real crypto-asset support, and does not list actual blockchain-based trading pairs or withdrawal addresses.

All trading occurs through centralized CFD infrastructure, where users speculate on price movements without owning underlying assets or controlling private keys.

Because BtcDana does not provide real Bitcoin custody or on-chain transactions, it **does not qualify as a Bitcoin wallet.**
