## App Description  

Aspen Digital is a full-service digital asset platform targeting HNWIs (High Net Worth Individuals), private wealth managers, and institutions. The Android app emphasizes market access, staking aggregation, quant strategies, structured products, venture deals, and portfolio tracking rather than self-custody wallet functionality.

The app's promotional materials showcase modules including Spot Account, Structured Account, Fund Account, Deal Account, PnL tracking, product discovery, DCA automation, and market intelligence. This positions it as an institutional broker/custodian platform rather than a consumer self-custody wallet.

## Analysis  

**1. Is it a wallet?**  
Operationally yes (deposits/withdrawals exist), but it functions as a custodial platform wallet where Aspen holds all private keys. The app provides account-based access to pooled digital assets rather than direct key control.

**2. Is it for bitcoins?**  
Yes. BTC is explicitly supported in trading pairs and structured products offered through the platform.

**3. Ability to send & receive**  
Functionally available through custodial deposit/withdrawal mechanisms, but subject to platform controls:
- **Deposits:** *"you may deposit digital assets into your Account with a specified address provided by us"* ([Terms §5.1.1](https://portal-ad.aspendigital.co/agreement))
- **Pooled custody:** *"digital assets may be pooled with the digital assets of other Users and may not be identifiable as separate"* ([Terms §5.1.4](https://portal-ad.aspendigital.co/agreement))
- **Withdrawals:** *"you may request withdrawal"* but Aspen sets limits and may decline withdrawals to high-risk addresses ([Terms §5.2.1-5.2.2](https://portal-ad.aspendigital.co/agreement))

**4. Key custody**  
Fully custodial. The Terms explicitly state:
- *"Aspen Digital shall maintain full custody of the digital assets, funds and User data/information"* ([Terms §13.3](https://portal-ad.aspendigital.co/agreement))
- Users hold account balances and claims, not private keys
- *"foundational virtual accounts, including main accounts and subaccounts, to record transactions [and] asset changes"* ([Terms §1.1.1](https://portal-ad.aspendigital.co/agreement))

**5. App Interface:** The app does not expose private keys, seed phrases, or typical self-custody wallet features (no address book, QR receive screens, seed backup/export). Instead, it focuses on broker/custodian-style portfolio management and investment products.

## Conclusion  

Aspen Digital is a regulated institutional digital asset platform that allows Bitcoin deposits and withdrawals but maintains **full custody** of all user funds and private keys. While it technically handles Bitcoin transactions, it operates as a custodial broker/investment platform rather than a self-custodial Bitcoin wallet.

Users interact with account balances and investment products through Aspen's controlled infrastructure, with all Bitcoin held in pooled custody. The app's focus on structured products, institutional features, and explicit custody controls in the Terms of Service clearly positions this as a **custodial** service, not a self-custody wallet solution.
