## App Description

Zerion is a self-custodial Web3 and DeFi wallet designed for managing tokens, NFTs, and DeFi positions across Ethereum, Solana, and 50+ other smart-contract blockchains. Zerion focuses on portfolio tracking, token swaps, DeFi discovery, and Ledger integration, positioning itself as a multi-chain hot wallet rather than a Bitcoin-native wallet.

## Analysis

**The app does not support native Bitcoin (BTC)**; Bitcoin exposure is limited to tokenized representations such as Wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC) on EVM-compatible networks.
