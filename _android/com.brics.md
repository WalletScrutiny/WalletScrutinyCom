## App Description

BRICS Pay Wallet is a multi-chain, self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet that allows users to store, send, receive, and manage digital assets across multiple blockchain networks from a single mobile application. The wallet supports Bitcoin along with many other cryptocurrencies such as Ethereum, USDT, BNB, XRP, Solana, TRON, and various ERC-20, TRC-20, and BEP-20 tokens. It also includes features for token swaps, fiat purchases, DeFi interactions, staking, NFT storage, and connecting to decentralized applications through WalletConnect.

## Testing and Analysis

We tested the app and posted the [screenshot on x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2029419439712813074). We were able to get the seed phrases which when exported to a third-party app generated a matching BTC address. However, no claims regarding source-availability have been made.
