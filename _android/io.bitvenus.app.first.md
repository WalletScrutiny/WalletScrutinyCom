## App Description

BVOX operates a cryptocurrency exchange that provides spot trading, leveraged perpetual futures, and copy-trading services across a large set of cryptoassets, explicitly including Bitcoin (BTC). User funds are deposited into platform-controlled wallets, with asset custody, trade execution, risk management, and withdrawals handled by BVOX’s internal systems rather than by user-held private keys. The app also offers fiat on-ramps, demo trading accounts, referral incentives, and security controls such as hot/cold wallet segregation and multi-factor authentication, positioning it as a full-service centralized trading platform.

This is a **custodial** service.
