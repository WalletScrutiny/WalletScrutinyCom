## App Description

A review of Play Store metadata, developer information, and historical Bitbns materials indicates that Bitbns Pay (com.bitbnspay) is an official application published by Buyhatke Internet Private Limited, the same company that operates the Bitbns exchange and publishes the primary Bitbns app (com.bitbns). Both applications list consistent corporate identifiers, including the official support domain (@bitbns.com) and matching registered company details.

Bitbns Pay appears to have been launched as a secondary or “lite” application, positioned historically for simpler buying, selling, and payment use cases, including fast UPI-based flows. This app is distinct from the main Bitbns exchange application, which is the primary product currently linked from bitbns.com. The existence of two separate app IDs reflects different product scopes rather than impersonation.

## Analysis

Bitbns Pay is described historically as a “lite” or payments app layered on top of the same exchange infrastructure, not as an independent self-custody wallet.
