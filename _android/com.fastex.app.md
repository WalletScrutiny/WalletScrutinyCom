## App Description

Fastex  is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange platform offering spot trading, market monitoring tools, and support for multiple crypto-fiat pairs.

The app supports Bitcoin, providing buy/sell functionality, spot BTC trading pairs, and BTC deposits and withdrawals.

It includes standard exchange features such as limit, stop-limit, market, and stop-market order types as well as optional trading API functionality.

Identity verification, custodial account balances, and multi-asset management are handled within the platform’s centralized exchange infrastructure.

## Analysis 

The terms [state that:](https://www.fastex.com/terms-and-conditions)

> “Fastex Services” means the services offered by us to you through the Platform which include the receipt & transmission of orders, execution of orders on behalf of other persons, custodian or nominee services, placing of virtual financial assets, the operation of a VFA exchange and transfer services as detailed in the relevant Costa Rica legislation.

This is a **custodial** app.
