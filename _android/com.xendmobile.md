## App Description

Xend Finance is an Android app that provides crypto savings and investment services, allowing users to deposit and withdraw supported digital assets through wallets and addresses generated by the platform.

## Analysis

We initially wanted to mark the app as custodial, but as we've discovered, it [actually does not support Bitcoin (BTC)](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2004441672193921389) through the Bitcoin network. 

The only networks available are BSC and the Ethereum network, making this app **not available as a Bitcoin wallet**.
