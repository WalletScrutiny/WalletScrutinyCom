## App Description

Parler Pay is a peer payment app and digital wallet for the Optio ecosystem.
The Google Play listing says users can send supported digital assets to other users.
It says the wallet can store and manage ETH, USDT, OPT, and other supported tokens.
It also says users can view and manage rewards generated through the Optio network.
The App Store listing repeats the same ETH, USDT, OPT, and Optio rewards claims.

## Analysis

Parler Pay is presented as a crypto wallet.
It supports peer-to-peer payments and token management.
However, the public listings do not claim Bitcoin support.
The named assets in the app-store listings are ETH, USDT, and OPT.
The Optio support page describes Optio as its own blockchain with a native OPT token.
That is not Bitcoin.

When we tried to access the app, Google Play reported that it was not available in our region.
This limits direct testing, but it does not change the Bitcoin-support finding.

The official Parler Pay support documentation is also focused on Optio assets.
The `What is Parler Pay?` support article says Parler Pay is integrated with the Optio Blockchain.
It says users can earn, store, and manage OPT digital rewards.
That same support article names other supported assets as USDC, USDT, ETH, and WBTC.
WBTC is wrapped Bitcoin.
It is not native Bitcoin mainnet support.
The `Why should I use Parler Pay?` support article says Parler Pay is where users hold and manage earned OPT digital rewards.
It says future features include sending, receiving, and converting OPT to fiat.
The `How to send OPT through Parler Pay` article instructs users to select the Optio wallet and send OPT.
The staking and lock-up support articles are also about OPT.

A search of the official Parler Pay support knowledge base for `bitcoin` returned no support articles.
A search of the same support knowledge base for `btc` also returned no support articles.
We found no official documentation saying Parler Pay supports BTC, Bitcoin mainnet, or Lightning.
For WalletScrutiny purposes, WBTC and vague `other supported tokens` language do not establish Bitcoin support.
The review stops at the Bitcoin-support step.

For WalletScrutiny purposes, the verdict is **nobtc**.
