## App Description

Intebix is presented as a cryptocurrency exchange for buying, selling, and trading digital assets including Bitcoin. Its Privacy Policy says the platform offers digital wallet services to registered users for storing digital currency traded on the platform. The Terms of Use describe a Hosted Wallet connected to the user's Intebix account.

## Analysis

The Terms of Use say the Hosted Wallet is provided by INTEBIX and/or third parties, and that INTEBIX may freeze a user's account and services. The Admission to Trading Rules explicitly refer to the "custody service of the company" and set limits on aggregated client funds the company may hold, including 50 BTC equivalent for Retail Clients and 1,250 BTC equivalent for Professional Clients. I found no public documentation showing user-held seed phrases, private-key export, or independent recovery outside the Intebix account system.

This app is **custodial** because access to funds depends on the provider-controlled hosted wallet service.
