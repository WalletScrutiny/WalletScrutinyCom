## App Description

Mercado Bitcoin (Portugal) is a cryptocurrency exchange application that enables users to buy, sell, and manage digital assets through a custodial account system. It supports Bitcoin (BTC) trading along with other cryptocurrencies offered on the platform. Users can deposit funds, execute market or limit orders, and monitor portfolio balances within the app interface. The service operates as an account-based trading platform rather than a self-custody wallet, with asset management handled by the provider.
