## App Description

The Google Play listing says the app supports BTC, ETH, LTC, and DOGE. It also lists buying cryptocurrency, locating BudgetCoinz ATMs, swapping cryptocurrency, and paper wallet transfer as app functions. BudgetCoinz help pages describe using the app to scan a Bitcoin paper wallet private key and move funds into the app wallet.

## Testing and Analysis

We tested the app and posted the [video on x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2050396726503280736).

BudgetCoinz documents paper-wallet sweeping by scanning a private key, but that only proves import of an external paper-wallet key. Our testing reveals that the 15-word seed phrase generated by the app can be exported to Electrum. The BTC address matches. 

The app is self-custodial, but offers no claims regarding source-availability. A search on GitHub for its potential repository using the appId `org.budgetcoinz.app`, [did not yield relevant results](https://github.com/search?q=org.budgetcoinz.app&type=code). 

This app is **not source-available**.
