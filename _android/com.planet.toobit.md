## App Description

Toobit is an exchange application that allows users to buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies through a centralized trading account, as stated in the Play Store listing. The app supports Bitcoin trading pairs, but it does not generate or store private keys on the user’s device, functioning strictly as a custodial exchange rather than a Bitcoin wallet. It provides access to spot trading, derivatives, and account-level controls tied to Toobit’s centralized infrastructure.

## Analysis 

> Toobit is one of the world's most secure crypto trading platforms. Powered by its proprietary Bee-Safe technology, Toobit provides full transparency and protection for your assets. Advanced security measures include 1:1 asset backing with regular Proof of Reserves audits, AES-256 encryption, cold storage, and dedicated risk control to safeguard your funds and personal data.

This is a **custodial** platform.
