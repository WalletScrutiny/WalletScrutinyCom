## App Description

Crystal Ball Markets MT7 is a CFD trading platform that offers speculative trading on cryptocurrencies, forex, commodities, stocks, and indices. The platform uses the Mobius Trader 7 system to provide leveraged trading with tight spreads and zero slippage execution. Users can trade cryptocurrency CFDs (contracts for difference) which track Bitcoin and other crypto prices without owning the underlying assets. The service is regulated by FINTRAC in Canada and FSA in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines.

## Analysis

1. **Is it a wallet?** No, Crystal Ball Markets is a CFD trading platform, not a cryptocurrency wallet.

2. **Is it for bitcoins?** Partially, it offers Bitcoin CFD trading but not actual Bitcoin ownership or transactions.

3. **Can it send and receive bitcoins?** No, while the platform accepts crypto deposits and withdrawals, these are converted to/from fiat for CFD trading purposes rather than actual Bitcoin transfers.

4. **Is the product self-custodial?** No, users do not hold actual cryptocurrencies, only trading positions on price movements.

5. **Is the source code publicly available?** No, there is no mention of open source code for this proprietary trading platform.

6. **Is the decompiled binary legible?** Not applicable, as the source code is not available.

7. **Is the source code of this wallet available?** No, Crystal Ball Markets does not provide public access to their trading platform source code.

## Verdict

Crystal Ball Markets cannot function as a Bitcoin wallet because it only offers CFD trading on cryptocurrency prices, not actual Bitcoin ownership or transfers. While the platform accepts cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals (including Bitcoin), these are converted to/from fiat currency for CFD margin trading purposes. Users trade "paper bitcoins" through contracts for difference that track Bitcoin's price movements but **cannot send or receive actual Bitcoin** to external addresses. 

This is confirmed by their official legal documentation:
- **[Product Disclosure Statement](https://res2.weblium.site/res/5e81a3cd78e60d0021ef4639/5eb27efbed14480022340d5d)** explicitly states CFDs are "without actually owning the underlying product" and "do not result in the physical delivery of the underlying instrument"
- **[Financial Services Guide](https://res2.weblium.site/res/5e81a3cd78e60d0021ef4639/5eb27edeed14480022340d06)** and **[Client Agreement](https://res2.weblium.site/res/5e81a3cd78e60d0021ef4639/5eb27ec6b543fa00218b2ba4)** focus exclusively on fiat margin accounts for CFD trading with no mention of cryptocurrency custody

This confirms Crystal Ball Markets is a derivatives trading platform rather than a Bitcoin wallet.
