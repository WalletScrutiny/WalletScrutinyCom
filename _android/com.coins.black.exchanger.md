## App Description

Coins.Black is an instant cryptocurrency exchanger supporting 25+ cryptocurrencies, 100+
banks, and 15+ payment systems including Russian banks, SWIFT, SEPA, and cash. Users select
a source currency and a destination currency, provide their own destination wallet address,
and send from an external wallet to a deposit address provided by the service.

## Analysis

Coins.Black has **no wallet** functionality. The mobile app is the mobile interface of the
website. The Terms of Service state explicitly:

> "Coins.Black only provides services for exchanging, buying and selling electronic currencies."

Users provide their own destination address and send funds from their own external wallet.
The service performs a one-time conversion and forwards the result. There is no account
balance, no stored funds, and no platform-held wallet. Investigation stops here.
