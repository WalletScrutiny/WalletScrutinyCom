## App Description

Coinfinity Wallet is an Austrian Bitcoin brokerage app that includes an integrated non-custodial Bitcoin wallet. The wallet uses a 12-word BIP39 recovery phrase, allowing users to back up and import their keys into other compatible wallets. Coinfinity GmbH is registered with the Austrian Financial Market Authority (FMA) as a Digital Asset Service Provider.
