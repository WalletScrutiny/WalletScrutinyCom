## App Description

Delta Exchange (India) is a custodial derivatives trading platform operated by an FIU-registered entity (REID: VA00041101) that offers BTC and ETH options with daily, weekly, and monthly expiries, along with perpetual futures on multiple assets.

All trading is INR-settled, and user balances remain custodied by Delta rather than generated through on-device private keys.

The platform provides derivatives-focused tools such as limit, market, stop, and bracket orders, strategy builders, and support for small-lot BTC/ETH contracts, subject to the exchange’s own custody and margining rules.

## Analysis

The Indian version of the [terms](https://www.delta.exchange/terms-of-use) for the site:

> “Account Equity” means the balance of INR reflected in our books and records as credited to your Account as calculated by us. Account Equity includes the margin used in existing positions and orders, margin available for trading and unrealized cashflows, i.e. cashflows that will take place when open positions are closed but does not include Trading Credits. Account Equity represents the total value of your funds (“Customer Record”) that are in our custody.

This is a **custodial** service.
