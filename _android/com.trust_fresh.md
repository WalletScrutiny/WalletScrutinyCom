## App Description

TrustNE Wallet is presented as a multi-chain wallet with Bitcoin support. The Google Play listing says users are the sole owners of their private keys and that seed phrase generation and storage happen on the device. The website says users can import or create wallets, including a Bitcoin wallet.

## Testing and Analysis

The Privacy Policy says wallet data, including private keys and transaction history, remains on the user's device. The Terms say users can create a wallet using a seed phrase, and also mention optional email-based recovery while stating that TrustNE does not store or manage private keys. 

We [tested the app](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2050494303970148501) and can confirm the claims.
