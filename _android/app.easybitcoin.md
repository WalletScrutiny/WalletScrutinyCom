## App Description

EasyBitcoin is a Bitcoin buying and rewards app operated under license from the easy® brand (of easyGroup). The service is powered by Uphold, which provides the trading infrastructure and regulatory framework for user accounts and custody. Users can buy, sell, and hold Bitcoin through Uphold’s custodial platform, but no self-custody or key export functionality is offered.

## Analysis

Section 3.4 of the [terms](https://easybitcoin.app/legal/terms)

> Digital Assets are held by Uphold primarily in cold storage, with limited amounts being kept in warm or hot wallets to enable liquidity for Users. Certain Assets (or portions thereof) may be held in accounts on trusted third party exchanges in the name of Uphold for the benefit of Users. 

This app offers a **custodial** service.
