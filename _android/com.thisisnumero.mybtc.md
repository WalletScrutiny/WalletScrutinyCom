## App Description

> My BTC is a free app that will transform the way you manage your prepaid and postpaid BTC services. Manage your prepaid and postpaid accounts from the comfort of your phone.
>
> - See detailed airtime and balance information for Prepaid including expiration dates
> - Easily purchase Prepaid Plans for superior voice, text and data value
> - Get instant access to billing information for Postpaid customers
> - Buy or transfer Top Up directly from the app
> - Pay your bill without waiting in line
> - Contact Customer Care using Online Chat
