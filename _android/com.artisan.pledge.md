## App Description

AScoin Wallet is a multi-chain self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet launched in 2020 that lets users create or import wallets via mnemonic seed phrases. It supports major networks including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum, BNB Chain, TRON, Solana, and Cardano. The app integrates over 20 DEX protocols such as Uniswap v2/v3, PancakeSwap, Curve, and SushiSwap for on-chain swaps.

## Analysis

The listed homepage `assure.pro` has privacy ssl-related warnings. Meanwhile, its privacy policy is hosted on another domain `assurepro.io/Assure/Websites/pro.html`. Accessing the root of the domain however, leads to a 404. 

We were able to [setup a Bitcoin wallet](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1988516763374420400) with provided seed phrases. We did not find claims proving it is source-available and no relevant results for its appID in [GitHub Code.](https://github.com/search?q=%22com.artisan.pledge%22&type=code)

We did not add the apple app, although branding materials seem to coincide because we did not find an authoritative website linking the identities of these apps. The apple app lists the domain `as-pro.io` in its privacy policy. Domain name inconsistency may be a reason for the user to be wary of this app.

This app is **not source-available**.
