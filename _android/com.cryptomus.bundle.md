## App Description

Cryptomus is a crypto-services platform that integrates trading, payments, P2P exchange, and multi-asset account management. Users can deposit, withdraw, convert, and transfer supported cryptocurrencies entirely through the provider’s infrastructure. The service also offers merchant payment functions, automatic conversion tools, whitelisted withdrawals, and account-level security features such as 2FA and PIN protection.
