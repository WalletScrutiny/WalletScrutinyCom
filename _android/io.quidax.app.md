## App Description

Quidax is a mobile app for a centralized cryptocurrency exchange that lets users buy, sell, swap, and hold Bitcoin and dozens of other cryptocurrencies using local bank transfers and in-app payment methods across several African countries.

## Analysis

According to the Google Play description

> all customers' crypto assets on Quidax are allocated to cold wallets (offline) and stored in line with the industry's best security practices.

This is a good indication that the service is **custodial**.
