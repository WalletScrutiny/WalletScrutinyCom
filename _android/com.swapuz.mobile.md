## App Description

Swapuz is an Android app that provides a custodial crypto exchange service, focused on fast swaps between 480+ coins without requiring an account or KYC in most cases. The app allows users to specify a deposit currency and a receiving address, then Swapuz executes the exchange and sends the requested asset. The service positions itself as a quick "swap gateway" rather than a wallet, with optional fixed/floating rates and built-in support via Telegram and email.

## Analysis

**Is it a wallet?**
No. Swapuz is an exchange/swap service, not a self-custodial wallet.

**Is it for bitcoins?**
Yes. Bitcoin is one of the supported assets among 480+ coins. Users can receive any cryptocurrency by providing their own address. 

## Verdict

Swapuz is not a Bitcoin wallet but rather a custodial crypto swap service. While it advertises anonymity and ease of use, users never control their own keys, and funds are temporarily held by the service during swaps. The app is active and maintained, but given its custodial design and lack of open-source code, it cannot be considered verifiable or reproducible under WalletScrutiny methodology. 

**Note:** Once the user sends the coins to be exchanged with them, for a brief moment, they are in custody of the coins. But for the purposes of this analysis, we will mark this as **not a wallet**.
