## App Description

BitSave is a crypto investment application that, according to the developer, offers structured investment products rather than a self-managed wallet. The developer states that the app provides products such as a Bitcoin-only investment, a crypto index product, and a hybrid crypto–gold product. BitSave is described as using third-party, regulated custodial infrastructure to store user assets, with features such as SIP-style investing, UPI deposits, and proof-of-reserves disclosures. 

## Analysis

The developer positions the app as a supervised and compliant platform where asset custody and transaction execution are handled by external regulated custodians rather than by the user.

BitSave [states](https://www.bitsave.club/post/bitsave-partners-up-with-liminal-for-digital-asset-custody-solution-to-enhance-security) that all user crypto assets are held under regulated third-party custodial infrastructure, with **custody provided by Liminal Custody Solutions**, rather than being controlled directly by users.
