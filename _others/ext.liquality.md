**Update 2026-10-30:** Liquality discontinued this browser extension on 2024-06-15 and asked users to move their seed phrase and private keys to other self-custodial wallets ([announcement](https://x.com/Liquality_io/status/1792678368694985162)). The extension is gone from the Chrome Web Store, and its [last release](https://github.com/liquality/wallet/releases/tag/v0.87.4) is from May 2023.
