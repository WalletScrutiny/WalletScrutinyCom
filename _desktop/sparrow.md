## App Description

> Sparrow Wallet is an open-source desktop Bitcoin wallet designed for advanced users who prioritize privacy, security, and transparency. It supports a wide range of hardware wallets and adheres to standards like PSBT (Partially Signed Bitcoin Transactions), making it suitable for complex setups including multi-signature configurations and air-gapped workflows.
>
> The wallet offers granular coin control, customizable fee settings, and the ability to connect to personal Electrum servers for enhanced privacy.
