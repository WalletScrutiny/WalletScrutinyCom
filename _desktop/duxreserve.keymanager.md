## Update 2025-10-06

Dux Reserve Key Manager was announced [March 2021](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2021/03/24/), but:

- There is no repo, binary, or download after 4 years.
- The official website no longer mentions the “Key Manager.”
- The company pivoted to a general “Dux Reserve” app still in early access.
- The founder Thibaud Maréchal now lists his position as PM of {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.bullbitcoin.mobile' verdict='true' %}
- We asked them on [x.com](https://x.com/dannybuntu/status/1975125739830993153) about the status of the app
- Given that the subdomain (beta.duxreserve.com) now redirects to an "Early access" page still with no download link and the fact that the founder is now the PM for another product, its fair to say that this is **vaporware.**

## 2025-04-25

As of this review there is no download or repository. There are no mentions of Key Manager, although there is an app on the site that remains in ["Early Access"](https://duxreserve.com/en/early-access)
