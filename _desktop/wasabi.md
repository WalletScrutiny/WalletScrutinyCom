## App Description

> Wasabi Wallet is a privacy-focused, open-source Bitcoin wallet for desktop, emphasizing non-custodial control and advanced anonymity features. It implements trustless coinjoins using the WabiSabi protocol, aiming to improve fungibility without compromising usability. Users retain full control of their keys, with built-in Tor integration and coin control tools. As a reproducibly built wallet, its binaries can be verified against source, contributing to transparency and security. Despite regulatory scrutiny of privacy-enhancing features, Wasabi continues to operate as a non-custodial wallet, leaving custody and compliance decisions to the user.

They have a guide on how to [build deterministically.](https://github.com/WalletWasabi/WalletWasabi/blob/master/WalletWasabi.Documentation/Guides/DeterministicBuildGuide.md)
