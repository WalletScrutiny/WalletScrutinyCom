## Notice

There's a notice on its homepage indicating that Infinity Wallet is "deprecated". However, we believe that their use of the word is incorrect, as they did not point to a newer app. We are marking it as defunct and with its **[source code no longer available](https://github.com/InfinityWallet/)**, we're modifying the verdict to reflect this. 

A copy of the notice:

```
Dear Visitor,


The Infinity Wallet software has been deprecated and is no longer supported. While the software may continue to function due to its non-custodial nature, functionality may at some time be degraded or no longer available. Any further usage continues to be at your own risk.

As a self-custodial wallet software, you hold your own crypto and control over your wallet. You can easily import your wallet for use in other compatible self-custodial wallet softwares using your 12 or 24 word seed phrase, or alternatively the private key for a coin.

The Infinity Wallet does not hold your crypto or operate any services. As a non-custodial wallet software, the Infinity Wallet is only a tool/interface to interact with public decentralized blockchains. Your crypto is under your complete control and management directly on the blockchain using any compatible non-custodial wallet softwares.

For a limited time if you have questions, you can reach out via chat or email.

```
