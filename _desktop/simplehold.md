**⚠️ Warning:** SimpleHold has closed. The team [announced](https://notum.ai/simplehold) that it is shutting SimpleHold down and moving to Notum, a portfolio management platform, and offers a tool there to export SimpleHold keys to other wallets. The simplehold.io website no longer loads, so the desktop app can no longer be downloaded.
