## App Description

Saymatik Web3 Wallet is published by scimatic. The Play Store listing describes it as a multichain Web3 wallet for storing, managing, and transacting with crypto assets across networks such as Scimatic, finance, ethereal, Avalanche, and Polygon. It also advertises token swaps, portfolio tracking, dApp access, token creation, NFT-related features, certificates, real estate tokens, and other Web3 tools.

The project's website presents Saymatik as a Web3 wallet and DeFi platform. The supported networks shown there are Binance, Ethereum, Scimatic, Avalanche, Polygon, Fantom, Cronos, and Arbitrum.

## Testing and Analysis

We reviewed the Play Store listing and the Saymatik website.

General cryptocurrency support, EVM networks, Web3 tokens, or DeFi features are not enough for a Bitcoin wallet verdict.

The Play Store description does not mention Bitcoin support. It describes a multichain Web3 wallet focused on Scimatic, Ethereum-like chains, Avalanche, Polygon, smart contracts, dApps, and token creation.

The Saymatik website also does not list Bitcoin as a supported network. Its visible supported networks are EVM/Web3-style networks such as Binance, Ethereum, Scimatic, Avalanche, Polygon, Fantom, Cronos, and Arbitrum.

We also tested the app and documented the result [here](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2056672581814718922).

At this stage, we found no evidence that Saymatik Web3 Wallet can create or import a native Bitcoin wallet, derive Bitcoin addresses, or send and receive on-chain BTC.

As a result, Saymatik Web3 Wallet does not meet WalletScrutiny's Bitcoin-wallet requirement and is classified as **nobtc**.
