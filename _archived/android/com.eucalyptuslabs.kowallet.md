## App Description

Koala Wallet is a non-custodial multi-chain wallet that supports assets and NFTs across networks such as Ethereum, Polygon, Kadena, and Solana, with local key generation and biometric unlock.

It integrates WalletConnect for interacting with Web3 dApps and provides native NFT display functionality for supported chains.

## Analysis

Our [tests](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1989227398508998993) show that the app offers only tokenized Bitcoin, **not real BTC on the Bitcoin network.**
