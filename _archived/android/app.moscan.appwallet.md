## App Description

MoWallet is a self-custodial, multi-chain wallet that supports Ethereum-compatible networks such as MO Chain, Binance Smart Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, and others. It enables users to manage ERC-20, ERC-721, and ERC-875 tokens, perform swaps, and interact with decentralized applications through an integrated browser. The app allows one-seed-per-wallet configuration to isolate accounts for improved security.

When we opened the app, we were given the chance to Select the Active Networks: Ethereum, MO Chain, Binance, Avalanche, Polygon, Fantom Opera, Heco, Arbitrum One, Klaytn Cypress, Gnosis - and **no BTC**.
