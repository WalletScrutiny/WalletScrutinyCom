## App Description

Bitcoin People BPay is a merchant-focused Android app that turns a mobile device into a Bitcoin point-of-sale (POS) for accepting payments via QR codes and managing transactions in real time.

The app does support Bitcoin, but it operates as a payment and settlement service where funds can be automatically converted to euros, indicating custodial handling rather than user-controlled private keys.

BPay is designed for businesses—not personal self-custody—emphasizing payment acceptance, fiat conversion, and integrations with e-commerce platforms.

## Analysis

BPay itself is not a general-purpose Bitcoin wallet: it does not provide in-app transaction signing or arbitrary outbound payments, and is designed primarily as a payment coordination and reporting tool rather than a user-controlled spending wallet.

**It is not a wallet**.
