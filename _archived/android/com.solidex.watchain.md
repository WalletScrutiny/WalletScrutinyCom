## App Description

This is a cryptocurrency portfolio tracker for Bitcoin and Ethereum addresses. Its linked website brands the app as **Quantum Code** and says it monitors wallet balances, USD values, transaction history, categories, and labels without account registration.

## Analysis

No reproducible-build test or hands-on APK test is recorded here.

The strongest available signal is the developer's own documentation. The terms describe the app as a read-only tracking tool. They state that it does not access private keys or seed phrases, cannot execute transactions, and is not a wallet service, exchange, or custodian.

The privacy policy says users provide wallet addresses and labels. It says data is stored with a device identifier on Firebase. It also says wallet addresses are shared with Blockchain.info and Etherscan to retrieve balance and transaction data.

The site and legal pages present this app as a watch-only portfolio tracker, not software that creates keys or signs Bitcoin transactions, so the defendable verdict is **not a wallet**.
