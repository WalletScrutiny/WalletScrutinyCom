## App Description

Caesium Wallet supports BNB Smart Chain tokens, other listed cryptocurrencies, and integrated NFT handling tied to “Alpha Returns” NFTs.

It provides a P2P order-posting and matching system and offers token swaps within its supported networks.

The app also advertises “global access” to assets across multiple supported cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin and Ethereum, based solely on claims made in its official description.

## Analysis

They claim in their Play page that: 

> Our versatile multi-currency wallet supports major cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and more, ensuring you stay connected to your assets.

However, our [testing](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1989159841680814442/photo/3) shows us that this app does **not support Bitcoin.**
