## App Description

HERE Wallet is a cryptocurrency wallet application focused on NEAR Protocol that functions as a universal hub to connect Binance accounts, on-chain wallets, and hardware wallets. The application supports swapping tokens via DEX and exchanges, bridging tokens through Binance accounts, staking on validators or liquid staking protocols, and gas-free USDT transfers on NEAR Protocol.
