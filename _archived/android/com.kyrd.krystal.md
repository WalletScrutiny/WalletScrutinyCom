## App Description

Krystal is a Web3 wallet developed by KYRD International Limited, focused on decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidity provisioning across decentralized exchanges (DEXes). 

The app claims to support "50+ blockchains" and allows users to "securely create or connect to your blockchain wallet", suggesting a non-custodial architecture where users control their private keys. 

The wallet specializes in providing analytics and management tools for liquidity providers on automated market makers (AMMs), with features for tracking and managing liquidity pool positions across multiple blockchain networks. 

The app description emphasizes DeFi functionality including cross-chain swaps, liquidity provisioning, and asset management, primarily targeting EVM-compatible blockchain ecosystems such as Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, Polygon, and similar networks.

## Analysis

There is no mention of Bitcoin support in any of its description. 

We can confirm that this app **does not support Bitcoin** as seen in our [testing](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1991044281960935862).
