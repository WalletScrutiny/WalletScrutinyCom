## App Description

It is a self-custodial wallet for the Canton network, with support framed around Canton, Ethereum, and Base, token transfers, swaps/bridges via USDCx, and passkey-secured keys that stay on-device.

## Analysis

Their website does not focus on the Canton network or on the app related to it, but a general Blockchain, Web3, and FinTech services firm. From the app description itself, we glean that this app **does not support Bitcoin**.
