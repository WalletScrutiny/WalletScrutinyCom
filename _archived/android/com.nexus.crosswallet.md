## App Description

CROSSx is NEXUS’s in-house wallet for the CROSS gaming ecosystem, described in the Play Store listing as a “self-custody wallet specifically designed for crypto-gamers” that lets users create a wallet in a few clicks, store Binance Smart Chain assets, and connect to multiple blockchain networks from one login session. The official product site reinforces that positioning by calling CROSS Wallet a “non-custodial EVM-compatible wallet with easy onboarding” that handles seamless send/receive actions inside the CROSS protocol.

## Analysis

Our [testing](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1991417449091330066) shows that this app **does not support Bitcoin**.
