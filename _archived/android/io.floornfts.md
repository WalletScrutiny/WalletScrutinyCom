## App Description

**Note: This app is [in the process of deprecation](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2004812436025889031) after OpenSea's [announcement](https://x.com/chrismaddern/status/1942586260377292854) of its acquisition**

According to its Google Play app description page:

It is a non-custodial mobile web3 trading and portfolio application that lets users connect their existing wallets to track, swap, and trade tokens and NFTs across multiple blockchains, including Ethereum, Solana, and Base.

## Analysis 

We [tested](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2004812436025889031) the app, and most of the functionalities are hidden behind a login page. Sign up is no longer possible. 

Its [support page](https://support.rally.xyz/en/articles/9376703-receiving-depositing-funds) **does not claim to support Bitcoin deposits**:

> ⚠️Note — Rally only supports ETH, Base, Arbitrum and Solana assets. Do not send other types of cryptocurrencies or assets to your wallet.
