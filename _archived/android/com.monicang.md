## App Description

The Play Store listing for Monica pitches the service as “Nigeria's fastest cryptocurrency exchange”, promising to convert Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum, Litecoin, Dogecoin, and other assets to Naira in about 60 seconds via instant bank transfers with “zero fees”.

Marketing copy highlights support for over 450,000 Nigerian users, ₦400B processed, Flexi bill-pay/airtime/data purchases, virtual USD cards, gift cards, and KYC onboarding that issues wallet addresses to receive crypto for automated cash-outs. 

Monica’s site at [monica.cash](https://monica.cash/) reinforces this positioning with repeated “convert crypto to Naira… lightning fast payouts to all Nigerian banks” messaging plus an emphasis on bank-grade security and 0% transfer fees. 

## Analysis

Available documentation only describes a custodial pipeline where users deposit BTC/USDT/ETH into Monica and the platform “instantly send or receive money… convert btc to Naira” with payouts to Nigerian banks ([monica.cash](https://monica.cash)). 

Neither the Play description nor the official site mention any ability to withdraw Bitcoin back to an external wallet, and the FAQ/marketing copy is entirely focused on exchanging crypto for fiat and paying local bills. 

Until on-chain withdrawals are documented or observed, Monica should be treated as a cash-out service rather than a wallet that allows users to hold or send their own BTC.

Strong proof in their terms: 

> 5) Service Description & Boundaries
>
> Off-ramp only. You send supported coin to a Monica-provided address; after network confirmation, we convert to NGN and pay out to your bank.
>
> No custody. We don’t hold user private keys or control your external wallets.

This is **not a bitcoin wallet** app.
