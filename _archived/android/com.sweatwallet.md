## App Description

Sweat Wallet is an Android app by Sweat Foundation that converts a user’s step count into the $SWEAT token and provides in-app features such as token swaps (via integrated DEX tools), “Grow Jars”, and governance voting.

## Analysis

The Play Store listing does not state that the app supports Bitcoin (BTC), and its described earn-and-use mechanics are centered on $SWEAT.

Despite the focus on $SWEAT, there are other coins available on the app, including: NEAR Protocol, BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum One. It only has WBTC (Wrapped Bitcoin).

**Bitcoin is not supported**.
