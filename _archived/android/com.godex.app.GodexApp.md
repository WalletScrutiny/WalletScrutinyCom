## App Description

Crypto Exchange – Godex is a cryptocurrency rate tracker and exchange application associated with the Godex.io platform. The app provides real-time price tracking for over 300 cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and other major digital assets. It includes charting tools, price alerts, currency conversion features, and portfolio tracking functionality.

In addition to market tracking, the application connects to the Godex exchange service, allowing users to swap supported cryptocurrencies at fixed exchange rates. The service operates as a non-custodial instant exchange model where users provide external wallet addresses for receiving swapped assets, rather than maintaining in-app custody accounts.

## Analysis

This is primarily an exchange service as seen in its ["how-it-works"](https://godex.io/how-it-works) section

> To exchange crypto with Godex, select the coins and amount, enter your destination wallet address, and send funds to the generated deposit address. After network confirmations, Godex swaps the assets at the selected rate and sends the converted cryptocurrency to your wallet. No registration or account creation is required.

This app is **not a wallet**.
