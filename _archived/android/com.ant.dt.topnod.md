## App Description

TopNod is a digital asset wallet that, according to the developer, focuses on managing and trading cryptocurrencies and real-world-asset (RWA)–linked tokens through a self-custody mobile application. The developer states that TopNod supports multiple blockchain networks, explicitly referencing Ethereum and Solana, and enables users to swap assets and interact with decentralized exchanges. The service is described as offering fiat on-ramps via payment methods such as Apple Pay and credit cards, along with access to promotional airdrop campaigns. The developer also claims the wallet uses a multi-shard access code mechanism for asset access management and positions TopNod as a tool to make Web3 and RWA-linked digital assets easier to use and manage.

## Analysis

TopNod is **not a native Bitcoin wallet**. Primary Networks: It focuses on Ethereum, Solana, and RWA-specific chains like Plume and Pharos.
