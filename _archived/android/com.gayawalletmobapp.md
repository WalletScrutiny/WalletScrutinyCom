## App Description and Analysis

Gaya Wallet is a multichain Web3 wallet designed to let users manage, send, receive, and swap crypto assets across networks such as Solana, EVM chains, XRPL, and Tron.

The app supports creating wallets via email or social login using MPC technology, as well as traditional seed phrases or private keys for users who prefer full self-custody.

Gaya includes features such as in-wallet swaps, cross-chain bridging, fiat on- and off-ramps, WalletConnect integration, and an in-app dApp browser for interacting with decentralized applications.

It positions itself as a flexible, feature-rich wallet that combines asset management, cross-chain functionality, and engagement features like referrals, tasks, and an AI assistant in a single mobile app.

From their [FAQ](https://gayawallet.com):

> We currently support Solana, Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum, XRPL, and Tron, with many more launching soon.

This app **does not support Bitcoin.**
