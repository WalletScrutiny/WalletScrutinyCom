## App Description

Sinbad claims to be a cryptocurrency trading application focused on the MENA region, providing a peer-to-peer marketplace for buying and selling USDT, Bitcoin, and other cryptocurrencies. The application enables users to send and receive cryptocurrency to contacts and includes cryptocurrency price charts for analysis. According to the developer's documentation, private keys are generated on the user's device, though this functionality is not accessible in the current beta release.

## Analysis

The developer's own documentation states that Sinbad is a *"semi-custodial wallet because service providers used by Sinbad retain some control over your funds."* The documentation explicitly notes: "Sinbad is not yet a self custody wallet" and "the private key is not yet available in our latest release." While the developers claim that keys are generated on the user's device and users can verify wallet balances independently, the inability to access or export private keys in the current version means users lack independent control over their funds.

Third-party service providers retain control over user funds, and private key access is listed as a future feature. Regardless of terminology used ("semi-custodial" or "non-custodial"), the current implementation does not provide users with full independent control of their cryptocurrency.
