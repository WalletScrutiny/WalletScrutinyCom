## App Description and Analysis

Rainbow is a self-custodial cryptocurrency wallet focused on the Ethereum ecosystem, designed to help users manage, send, receive, and trade Ethereum-based assets across mainnet and multiple Layer-2 networks including Optimism, Arbitrum, Polygon, Base, and Zora.

The app allows users to buy crypto, swap and bridge tokens, import existing wallets via seed phrases, and interact with decentralized applications using WalletConnect.

Rainbow emphasizes user ownership by ensuring assets are held directly by the user through cryptographic keys rather than by the app or a centralized exchange.

It is open source and positions itself as a user-friendly yet powerful Ethereum wallet with strong support for NFTs, ENS, DeFi, and on-chain activity discovery.

Rainbow does not generate Bitcoin addresses, does not connect to the Bitcoin network, and cannot send or receive native BTC (addresses starting with 1, 3, or bc1). Rainbow’s own product positioning and documentation describe it as an Ethereum-first wallet focused on Ethereum and compatible networks, and thus **does not support a Bitcoin wallet**.
