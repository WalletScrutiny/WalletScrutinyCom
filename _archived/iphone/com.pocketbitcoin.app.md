## App Description

Pocket Bitcoin is an app for purchasing Bitcoin through the Pocket Bitcoin service, which requires creating an account and linking a Lightning or on-chain Bitcoin address for payouts, according to the Play Store listing and official FAQ. The app itself does not generate or store private keys; instead, users must supply an external wallet address where purchased BTC is automatically sent, confirming it is not a Bitcoin wallet. All buying, bank-transfer processing, and payout mechanisms operate through Pocket Bitcoin’s custodial infrastructure until the payout is executed.

## Analysis

From the [FAQ](https://pocketbitcoin.com/faq/what-is-pocket):

> Pocket allows you to buy bitcoin once or recurringly from your bank account and receive your coins directly into self-custody on a Bitcoin wallet of your choice.

Users have to link their own wallet since **the app does not provide a wallet**.
