## App Description

Athena Bitcoin is a utility app for locating and navigating to Athena Bitcoin ATMs, which allow users to buy Bitcoin with cash. The app provides real-time ATM locations, directions, and promotional updates, but it does **NOT** include a Bitcoin wallet or seed-phrase functionality.

## Analysis

Although some of the phrases in the description seem to describe a wallet, such as: 

> At Athena Bitcoin, we empower you to take full control of your Bitcoin by ensuring you hold the private keys to your wallet. Your crypto, your rules.

In their FAQ, ["Instructions for Using an Athena App"](https://athenabitcoin.com/how-to-buy)

> Step One: Get a Wallet App On Your Smart Phone
> Don’t have a wallet installed yet? For a quick suggestion try the Edge wallet available for Android and Apple iOS. For more wallet suggestions, see this article:
>
> Our Most Recommended Bitcoin Wallets

They are recommending other wallet apps which means that this is **not a wallet app**.
