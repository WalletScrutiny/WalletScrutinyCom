## App Description

Blocto is a multi-chain crypto wallet that supports ecosystems such as Aptos, Solana, Flow, Polygon, and other EVM-compatible networks, according to the Play Store listing. The app provides features including asset management, NFT viewing, Web3 access, and an email-based onboarding system. It also integrates BloctoSwap, a decentralized exchange for supported chains.
