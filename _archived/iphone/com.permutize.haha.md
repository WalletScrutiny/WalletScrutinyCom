## Analysis 

We signed up with the service after install. Wallet creation involves the following under Mainnet:

- Polygon
- Rose
- Ethereum
- BSC
- Arbitrum
- Avalanche

We were not able to find the ability to add a bitcoin wallet. This app does **not have a bitcoin wallet**.
