## App Description

STO Wallet is an Android crypto application that claims to combine asset storage, staking, swaps, and trading of security tokens (STO) and real-world assets (RWA) within a single platform.

It claims to support Bitcoin (BTC) alongside Ethereum and its native STOC token, positioning itself as an alternative to centralized exchanges for holding and using these assets.

> Supports storage, staking, and swap services for major cryptocurrency assets such as BTC, ETH and STOC.

## Analysis

Despite their claims to support Bitcoin, we [tested](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2000741491644489982) the app and **did not find support for Bitcoin**.
