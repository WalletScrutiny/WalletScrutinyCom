## App Description

Bitstack is a custodial, regulated Bitcoin savings and investment app that allows users to buy, sell, send, and receive Bitcoin through Bitstack’s centralized infrastructure rather than a user-controlled wallet.

The service automates Bitcoin purchases through round-ups, recurring buys, and instant card purchases, all of which require users to operate within Bitstack’s licensed custodial framework under French AMF and ACPR regulation.

Bitcoin balances in the app are held by Bitstack (as an agent of Xpollens, an EU electronic-money institution), which manages custody, transfers, and compliance obligations on behalf of users.

## Analysis

This is a [**custodial**](https://bitstack-app.com/en/terms-conditions/conditions-generales-de-vente#deadLink) service: 

> ​•​the safekeeping and administration of crypto-assets on behalf of customers; (Article 3 (16) (a) MiCA), i.e. "the custody or control, on behalf of customers, of crypto-assets or the means of access to these crypto-assets, where appropriate in the form of private cryptographic keys".
