**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its [archived store page](http://web.archive.org/web/20210119091954/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coinapps.flash.wallet.android) from January 2021, by Flashcoin, described a FLASH, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin and Dash wallet with Google 2FA and a PIN, where you log in "with your existing multicurrency wallet account" at flashcoin.io. The [archived web wallet](http://web.archive.org/web/20201113071358/https://wallet.flashcoin.io/) also worked with email accounts. Neither page says who holds the keys or mentions a backup, so we cannot tell whether it was custodial and it stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %}. wallet.flashcoin.io no longer resolves and www.flashcoin.io no longer loads over HTTPS.

**Update 2021-10-07**: This app is not on Play Store anymore.
