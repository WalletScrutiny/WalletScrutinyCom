**Update 2026-10-30:** In July 2025 Bitrefill [renamed "Bitrefill Balance" to "Store Credit"](https://www.bitrefill.com/blog/updated-bitrefill-balance-to-store-credit/) and wrote: "Store Credit is non-withdrawable". You can [top it up with BTC](https://help.bitrefill.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402564626450-How-to-add-store-credit-by-paying-with-tokens-from-my-wallet) but only spend it on Bitrefill purchases. A balance you can top up but never get out again is not a wallet, so both apps change from {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %} to {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nowallet" type='short' %}.

## Android

**Update 2021-09-14**: This app was updated after a long time which had it fall
into our stale category but it remains being a custodial product.

While the primary purpose of this app is to buy stuff with Bitcoin and it appears
to be possible to use the app without putting money into it, the app also can
hold a balance, so it appears to be a wallet. At least we take that from the
screenshots.

As the description has no claims to the contrary and we can't find anything about
the app on their website except for a link to the Playstore, we have to assume
it is a custodial service.

Our verdict: The app is **not verifiable**.
