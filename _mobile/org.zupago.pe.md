**Update 2026-10-30:** The domain zupago.pe no longer belongs to the wallet: from 2021 the archived copies show a Peruvian finance blog, so we now link an [archived copy from 2018](https://web.archive.org/web/20181108075215/https://zupago.pe/). Its [archived FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20190511170846/https://zupago.pe/faq) describes an account holding "USD, EUR, and GBP" balances next to Bitcoin accounts, where money sent to you "will instantly appear into your ZuPago HyBrid (HD) Wallet balance", so we set the verdict to {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The ZuPago brand lives on as ViZO, listed as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/app.zupago.app' verdict='true' %}.
