**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. The domain
zupago.pe no longer belongs to the wallet: from 2021 the archived copies show a
Peruvian finance blog, so we now link an
[archived copy from 2018](https://web.archive.org/web/20181108075215/https://zupago.pe/)
of the "ZuPago HyBrid (HD) Wallet" with a London address. Its
[archived FAQ](https://web.archive.org/web/20190511170846/https://zupago.pe/faq)
describes an account you sign up for that holds "USD, EUR, and GBP" balances next to
Bitcoin, Bitcoin-Cash and Ethereum accounts, where money sent to you "will instantly
appear into your ZuPago HyBrid (HD) Wallet balance". That is a custodial service.
The ZuPago brand lives on as ViZO: zupago.app now redirects to vizo.app, and its app
{% include walletLink.html wallet='android/app.zupago.app' verdict='true' %}
is also custodial. We set the verdict to
{% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
