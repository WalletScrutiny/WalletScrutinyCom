## App Description

NovaDAX is a Brazilian cryptocurrency exchange app published by NOVADAX BRASIL PAGAMENTOS LTDA, shipping under the same identifier `com.nova.dax` on both Android and iOS. Its store listings describe trading in more than 200 digital currencies across roughly 300 pairs quoted against the Brazilian real (BRL), Bitcoin, Ethereum and Tether. They advertise fees from 0.07%, real-time charts, an order book and stop-limit orders, alongside staking, lending and an "Agent" mode.

## Testing and Analysis

We did not test this app hands-on: NovaDAX stopped accepting new registrations when it announced its wind-down, so an account can no longer be opened.

On 8 June 2026 the provider published [Descontinuação da Plataforma NovaDAX](https://www.novadax.com.br/suporte/An%C3%BAncios/detail/article/840061-Descontinua%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-Plataforma-NovaDAX), closing its Brazilian operation on a phased schedule. Deposits ended 12 June and order-book sales 30 June; **crypto withdrawals close 30 August 2026 and BRL withdrawals 30 September 2026.** Anyone still holding a balance should withdraw before those dates.

The wind-down itself shows the service is custodial: only an operator holding the coins can set a deadline by which users must remove them. The announcement speaks of safeguarding customer assets — *"os ativos dos clientes permanecem seguros e acessíveis durante todo o período de transição"* — and the listings describe order-book trading with platform-mediated deposits and withdrawals, never a recovery phrase or key export.

**Verdict: custodial.** NovaDAX holds the keys, so users do not control their funds. The review ends there — no user-held key material exists to audit, and reproducibility does not arise.

*A previous version of this review cited NovaDAX's security page for the claim that "99% of cryptocurrencies are stored in a multi-signed 'Cold Wallet' (offline)". That page now 404s with no Wayback snapshot, so it is no longer verifiable.*
