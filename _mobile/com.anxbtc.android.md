**⚠️ Warning:** ANX is gone. The ANXPro platform [ceased operations on 2021-09-30](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120073329/https://www.anxpro-closing.com/), and both [anxpro.com](https://anxpro.com/) and [anxintl.com](https://anxintl.com/) are now parked domains.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The provider shut down, so we mark this app defunct. The [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20151121065159/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.anxbtc.android) shows it was {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}: it showed "all transactions on customer's ANX account", let users set a daily limit for withdrawals, and let them revoke send privileges from the website if the device was lost. The closure notice asked users to withdraw before 2021-09-30 and charges the higher of 1% or USD 100 for later withdrawals.

On the main site there is a [announcement that the provider is closing](https://web.archive.org/web/20220120073329/https://www.anxpro-closing.com/) and all funds should be withdrawn from its services.<br>
It would be prudent to believe the wallet is no longer supported.
