## App Description

Money Partners Crypto Trade is an Android application by Money Partners Co., Ltd. that enables leveraged CFD trading on crypto assets. Supported pairs include BTC/JPY, ETH/JPY, BCH/JPY, and LTC/JPY with up to 2× leverage. The app provides real-time charts, rate monitoring, and multiple order types, but requires a Money Partners FX account to trade.

## Analysis

1. **Is it a wallet?** No. It is a CFD trading platform, not a wallet.

2. **Is it for bitcoins?** Yes. Supported assets include Bitcoin (BTC/JPY) along with ETH, BCH, and LTC.

3. **Can it send and receive bitcoins?** No. [FAQ](https://www.moneypartners.co.jp/support/index.html?anchor=faq) explicitly states: *"We do not handle physical crypto assets."*

4. **Is the product self-custodial?** No. It is custodial and operates via derivative contracts, not actual on-chain crypto.

## Verdict

Money Partners Crypto Trade is not a Bitcoin wallet but a custodial CFD trading platform for leveraged speculation. It **does not allow deposits or withdrawals of actual crypto assets**, and users cannot hold their own keys. The FAQ explicitly states "We do not handle physical crypto assets," confirming this is purely derivatives trading. The product is active and updated, but it cannot be considered self-custodial or verifiable.
