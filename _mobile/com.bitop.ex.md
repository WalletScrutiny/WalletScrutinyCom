## App Description

Bitop is the mobile trading interface of the centralized cryptocurrency exchange Bitop Exchange, which provides user access to spot trading, futures contracts, and custodial asset management.

The app does not generate or store private keys locally on the device; instead, all balances, deposits, withdrawals, and asset custody are managed by the Bitop exchange backend. Trading requires a registered Bitop account with KYC verification, and all transactions occur on Bitop-managed infrastructure rather than on-chain keys controlled by the user.
