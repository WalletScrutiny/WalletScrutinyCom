## App Description

Bitdenex is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange app that allows users to buy, sell, and hold digital assets including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Ripple (XRP), and its own Bitdenex token (BDNX). The app provides access to over 200 listed cryptocurrencies and includes market-monitoring tools such as price tracking and charting features.

All trading and custody operations occur under Bitdenex’s regulated exchange infrastructure, requiring full KYC verification before use 

## Analysis

From its [downloadable terms and conditions:](https://bitdenex.com/Documents/EU_Bitdenex_Terms_of_Service_V.01.09.2025..pdf#deadLink) 

**Section E 19 a:**

> Buy and Sell Service: buy and sell Crypto-assets for other Crypto-assets or funds, with Bitdenex acting as the counterparty for each trade. Clients are provided with a custodial wallet for which Bitdenex acts as the custodian. Crypto-assets can be bought through Bitdenex’s Buy and Sell Service using Crypto-assets held in the custodial wallet or Bitdenex Credits (as defined hereinafter), and sold through Bitdenex’s Buy and Sell Service for other Crypto-assets or Bitdenex Credits; 

This is a **custodial** service.
