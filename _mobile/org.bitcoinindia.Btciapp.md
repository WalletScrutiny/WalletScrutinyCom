## App Description

The Play Store listing describes it as a crypto app for Dogecoin mining, buying and selling crypto, and storing more than 100 cryptocurrencies in one app.

## Analysis

The app makes broad crypto claims on Google Play:

> Bitcoin India - Mine DOGE, Buy, Sell & Securely Store 100+ Cryptos

It also claims users can:

> mine Dogecoin (DOGE) directly from your device, buy Bitcoin (BTC), sell Ethereum (ETH)

The listing says assets are kept in an in-app wallet:

> Keep your digital assets safe in our in-app wallet

It also says the wallet uses:

> multi-layer authentication, and cold storage solutions.

We could not install the app and were not able to test it directly. I found no public documentation proving that users can send and receive Bitcoin on-chain. The linked website and privacy page did not provide clear wallet documentation, and I found no public source repository.

The app claims Bitcoin buying and in-app storage, but it does not clearly prove Bitcoin send and receive support. Pending the ability to test this further, this app is **not verifiable as a Bitcoin wallet that can send and receive Bitcoin**.
