## App Description

This is the current app of FameEX, a crypto exchange for spot, futures, copy and demo trading. It replaces the earlier FameEX apps that we list as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.app.fameex' verdict='true' %}. The app description says FameEX has "3 million global users", lets users "Trade 170+ major cryptocurrencies", and invites them to "claim free futures trial funds" and "withdraw your profits instantly".

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The app is titled "FameEX: Buy Bitcoin & Crypto", and the [FameEX website](https://www.fameex.com) offers BTC spot and futures markets.
- **Sending and receiving.** FameEX's help page on [withdrawal limits](https://www.fameex.com/en-US/support/crypto-deposit-withdrawal/what-is-your-withdrawal-limit) describes a "Minimum Withdrawal Amount" that "varies depending on the cryptocurrency and blockchain network", so users can withdraw coins to other wallets.
- **Custody.** The [FameEX terms](https://www.fameex.com/en-US/terms) say: "FameEX reserves full custody of all digital assets, funds, and user data/information in the FameEX account". They also say FameEX may "suspend your FameEX account ... for any reason, freeze or lock all digital assets or funds in such accounts".
- **History.** In January 2025 FameEX [announced a rebranding](https://www.fameex.com/en-US/news/fameex-unveils-2025-new-rebranding-initiative-to-usher-in-a-new-era-of-crypto-trading) with a new logo and visual identity. This app was released a few months later, in April 2025.

FameEX holds the coins of its users, so this app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, like the app it replaces.

## iPhone

The iPhone app is published by "FameX Ltd" rather than the Android publisher, FAMEEX INTERNATIONAL PTY LTD. It is the same FameEX exchange and account, so the same verdict applies.
