**⚠️ Warning:** MYKEY shut down its wallet app and all account services on
June 30, 2022.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. The pinned post on
[MYKEY Lab's X account](https://x.com/mykey_lab) says "MYKEY will be halting the
wallet APP and all related account services for all users due to policy reasons
from June 30, 2022. Please sign in and withdraw your assets by the deadline". Its
website mykey.org and its support site no longer respond. We never analyzed the app,
so it stays without a verdict.
