**⚠️ Warning:** CrossTower appears to be gone. Its website no longer resolves, and its Bermuda exchange was wound up in January 2024.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play, and crosstower.com no longer resolves, and [CrossTower Bermuda was wound up](https://www.royalgazette.com/international-business/business/article/20240123/crosstower-bermuda-is-latest-crypto-casualty/) by the Supreme Court of Bermuda in January 2024 because it could not pay its debts, so we mark it defunct. Its [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210622152617/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ctower.mobile) describes an exchange app to "buy and exchange bitcoin", with access through "API keys and PIN-code" and "highly protected cold storage to keep your Bitcoins SAFU". That is an exchange holding its users' coins, like CrossTower's Indian app {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.crosstower.india' verdict='true' %}, so we change the verdict from {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %} to {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
