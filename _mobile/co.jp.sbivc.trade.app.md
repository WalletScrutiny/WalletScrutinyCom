## App Description

This is the "VCTRADE" app of SBI VC Trade Co., Ltd., the SBI Group's Japanese crypto exchange, registered as a "Crypto Asset Exchange Business Operator" with the Kanto Local Finance Bureau (No. 00011). It trades over thirty crypto assets and stablecoins including Bitcoin, and offers staking, coin lending and recurring purchases. The Play Store description lists free "Cryptocurrency and Electronic Payment Method Deposit/Send" and warns: "In the event of our bankruptcy, we may be unable to return deposited funds, cryptocurrencies, and electronic payment methods." The same company also publishes the [BITPOINT app](/mobile/jp.co.bitpoint.bitpoint/), and it published an older VCTRADE app that we list as [旧バージョン：SBI VCTRADE mobile](/mobile/com.taotao_ex.android/) (custodial).

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play Store description lists "BTC (Bitcoin)" first among its assets. SBI VC Trade's [deposit and withdrawal page](https://www.sbivc.co.jp/services/transfer) lists "ビットコイン（BTC）" on the "Bitcoin" network.
- **Sending and receiving.** The same page says: "SBI VCトレードでは入庫と出庫、どちらも対応しています" ("SBI VC Trade supports both deposits and withdrawals"), for example "取引所から自分のウォレットに移動して長期保管したりする" ("moving assets from the exchange to your own wallet for long-term storage"). It adds that "暗号資産の受取・送付（入出庫）手数料はかかりません" ("there are no fees for receiving or sending crypto assets").
- **Custody.** SBI VC Trade's page on [safe trading](https://www.sbivc.co.jp/crypto-assets/efforts-for-safe-trading) says: "お客様の暗号資産及び電子決済手段は、お客様専用のウォレットにて、当社の資産とは分別管理いたします" ("customers' crypto assets and electronic payment methods are managed in wallets dedicated to customers, separately from the company's assets"). It also says assets are kept in "外部インターネットにつながらないコールドウォレット" ("cold wallets not connected to the internet") and that "暗号資産及び電子決済手段の秘密鍵は、当社管理基準に基づき、一つのアドレスに複数の秘密鍵を割り当てるマルチシグネチャアドレスを採用しております" ("for the private keys of crypto assets and electronic payment methods, we use multi-signature addresses that assign several private keys to one address, based on our management standards"). The company manages the keys.

SBI VC Trade stores customers' crypto in wallets whose keys it manages, so the app is [custodial](/methodology/#custodial).
