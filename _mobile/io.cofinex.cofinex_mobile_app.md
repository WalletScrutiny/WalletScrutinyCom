## App Description

Cofinex is a crypto exchange for Indian and European customers. Besides crypto it offers trading in US stocks and gold, copy trading, staking, a launchpad and a "CoPay Card". The Play description lists "Bitcoin (BTC)" first among its popular assets, along with "Secure Crypto Wallet" and "Fast INR Deposits & Withdrawals". The website footer says "Cofinex India ... is registered with FIU-IND as a Reporting Entity under the Virtual Asset Service Provider category" (VA00049334). It also says Minos Blockchain s.r.o. serves EU customers under a Czech National Bank authorisation for "virtual asset exchange and wallet custodian services". This app replaces the earlier Cofinex app, {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.app.cofinexexchange34' verdict='true' %}.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play description lists Bitcoin, and the website has a BTC/USDT spot market.
- **Deposits and withdrawals.** The [user agreement](https://cofinex.io/en/content/user-agreement) says that "you may withdraw your Digital Assets by sending it to a different blockchain address controlled by you or a third party", and it covers transfers sent "to an Account from an external wallet not hosted on the Platform".
- **Custody.** The same user agreement says: "All Digital Assets held in your Account are held by us on a custodial basis" and "We maintain full custody of the Digital Assets, funds and User information/data". The [terms](https://cofinex.io/en/content/terms-conditions) list "Digital Asset Custody" among the services.

Cofinex holds the bitcoin for its customers, so this app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, like the app it replaces.

## iPhone

The iOS app has a different bundle ID and is published by Cofinex Technologies private limited. It gives access to the same Cofinex exchange account, so the same verdict applies.
