## Android

## App Description

XSPA AI presents itself as an AI-driven crypto-related application and claims to function as a non-custodial wallet, according to its Google Play description.

## Analysis

We [tested](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2004454168225108014) the app and confirm the provision of a BTC address as well as the seed phrases. Once imported into a third-party app such as Electrum, [the seed phrases generated a BTC address](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2004455274841886791) that matched the one provided by the app. 

Apart from not finding claims regarding source-availability, a search for the app ID on GitHub [did not reveal any relevant Android repository coinciding with the app](https://github.com/search?q=%22com.xspa.ai%22&type=code). 

**This app is not source available**.
