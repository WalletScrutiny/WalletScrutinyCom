**Update 2026-10-30:** Upbit is still {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
On 2025-11-27 hackers took about 54 billion won (USD 36 million)
[from a hot wallet](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/11/28/south-korea-suspects-north-korea-linked-lazarus-behind-usd36m-upbit-hack),
and Korean authorities suspect the Lazarus group. Upbit kept 98.33% of assets in
cold wallets at the end of October 2025 and
[plans to reach 99%](https://www.sedaily.com/NewsView/2H1OE33RXA). Naver
Financial agreed to take over Dunamu, Upbit's operator, in November 2025; the
share swap still waits for regulators and was
[postponed to March 2027](https://en.sedaily.com/news/2026/10/07/naver-financial-dunamu-share-swap-delayed-again-to-march).

There are two versions of this app: one for Korea and one that's global. 

Upbit uses ID verification and KYC therefore we can assume that it is **custodial** and **not verifiable**. You cannot deposit or withdraw BTC as well as other altcoins without finishing their level 2 KYC. They also claim to have multi-sig and cold wallets.
