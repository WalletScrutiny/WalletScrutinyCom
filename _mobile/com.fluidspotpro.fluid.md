## App Description

Fluid is published on Google Play by "Fluid Android". It describes itself as a "crypto exchange platform to track, buy, and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin, and more". Version 1.0.1, the only update listed (November 2023), offers price tracking, a portfolio overview and crypto news. The description says the actual wallet is not available yet: "Sign up for Early Access, The Fluid Wallet coming Soon !!"

## Analysis

- **No wallet in the app.** Apart from the wallet that is "coming Soon", the features listed are price tracking ("Search for real-time Crypto prices"), a "Quick Overview of your Crypto Portfolio" and "Crypto News". The description does not explain how to buy, sell, deposit or withdraw anything.
- **Website unreachable.** The developer website listed on Google Play, `fluidspot.pro`, does not resolve in DNS, and neither does the domain of the support email, `fluid-spot.com`. We could not find terms of service or a help centre.
- The linked [privacy policy](https://www.privacypolicies.com/live/5331ad6a-97ad-46fd-bc6d-2f120a3fa4e1) was made with a generator, dates from October 14, 2023, and only names "Fluid" in "Missouri, United States" as the company. It says nothing about holding funds.
- The app has not been updated since November 2023.

By its own description the wallet has not been released, and the app offers price tracking and news only. We found no evidence that it can hold or move funds, so it is [not a wallet](/methodology/#nowallet).

**Not a wallet**
