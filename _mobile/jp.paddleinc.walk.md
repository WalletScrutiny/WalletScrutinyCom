## App Description

BitWalk is a step-counting rewards app by Paddle, Inc. ("Paddle Co., Ltd." in its terms) of Japan. Users earn small amounts of bitcoin for walking, funded by "a portion of the advertising revenue it receives from advertisers". Earnings are recorded in an in-app "Bitcoin Ledger", and once a minimum amount has built up, they can be transferred out to an external crypto account. The app supports only bitcoin.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** Rewards are paid in BTC. The [Terms of Service](https://bitwalk.jp/term_of_use) define the "Bitcoin Ledger" as the place where "Members can view their current amount of Bitcoin and the history of obtaining Bitcoin."
- **Withdrawal only, at the provider's discretion.** Article 11 of the terms says: "We will remit Bitcoin to an external third-party virtual currency wallet after confirming the Member's identity using the Bitcoin Ledger." It adds: "We have sole discretion on which partners we will use for such virtual currency wallets." Article 1 says that after a certain amount is accumulated, "a Member is able to transfer it to their external third-party account." The terms describe no way to deposit bitcoin. Article 10 says rewards cannot be transferred between members.
- **Custody.** Paddle keeps the ledger and decides on every payout. Article 10 says bitcoin is awarded "solely in the manner and amount we determine". Article 12 lists cases where "we may take away the Bitcoin accumulated in the Member's Bitcoin Ledger", including when "We determine that it is appropriate to take away Bitcoin granted to a Member." Article 8 says members who cancel their membership "will lose all Bitcoin awarded".
- The landing page [lp.bitwalk.jp](https://lp.bitwalk.jp/) has no information about payouts or custody.

BitWalk holds an earned bitcoin balance for the user and Paddle decides how much of it the user gets to keep, up to taking it away. Bitcoin can leave the app as a payout to an external wallet but cannot be deposited. As with other rewards apps that pay out bitcoin, such as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/io.gosats' verdict='true' %}, we rate it [custodial](/methodology/#custodial).
