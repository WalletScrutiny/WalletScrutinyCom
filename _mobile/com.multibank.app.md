## App Description

MultiBank.io is a cryptocurrency exchange platform operated by MultiBank Group, a financial services company established in 2005. The platform offers Bitcoin and cryptocurrency trading with features including leverage trading (up to 250x), instant purchase via payment cards, and cold storage security. MultiBank Group operates under 15+ regulatory licenses worldwide including ASIC (Australia), BaFin (Germany), CySEC (Cyprus), MAS (Singapore), and VARA (UAE).

## Analysis

The service uses [cold storage](https://multibank.io/support/faq/privacy-and-security/security-and-regulation) and is therefore **custodial**.
