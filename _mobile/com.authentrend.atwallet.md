**Update 2026-10-30:** AuthenTrend [ended the AT.Wallet service on 2025-06-01](https://authentrend.com/blog/important-notice-atwallet-service-termination-on-june-1-2025) because the third-party transaction server it relied on was shut down. Sales of the {% include walletLink.html wallet='hardware/atwallet' verdict='true' %} card had already stopped in May 2023. AuthenTrend advises users to restore their recovery phrase in another wallet.
