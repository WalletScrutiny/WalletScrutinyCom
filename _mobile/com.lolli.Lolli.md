## App Description

Lolli, by Lolli Holdings, Inc., is a US rewards app. Users earn bitcoin or cash for playing partner games in the "Lolli Arcade" and claiming daily prizes. The website and browser extension also pay shopping cashback. Lolli holds the rewards and pays them out on request. The current Play Store description says: "Withdrawals are on pause for now. Don't worry, your rewards are safe and now that we've moved to our new infrastructure we are expecting withdrawals to return in October!"

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** Rewards can be earned in bitcoin. The [help page](https://www.lolli.com/help) says withdrawals go "over the Lightning Network with a minimum of just 1,000 sats" or to Mezo "once you reach 0.0003 BTC (30,000 sats)". The Mezo option "requires an Ethereum or Mezo wallet", so it pays out on another network and not as native BTC. Only the Lightning option sends actual bitcoin.
- **Custody.** The help page says: "Lolli will securely store all your funds with our custodial service." It also warns that "Rewards are not a bank balance or stored value". Confirmed rewards "are forfeited automatically" after 12 months without a qualifying activity, and "can be forfeited if your account is terminated."
- **No deposits.** The help page describes no way to deposit bitcoin into Lolli. A balance comes only from rewards, and the only way out is a withdrawal.
- **Withdrawals paused.** The Play Store description, current for version 4.3.10, says withdrawals are on pause.

Lolli holds the bitcoin for the user but accepts no bitcoin deposits. Its Lightning withdrawal is a reward payout and is currently paused. The app cannot send and receive bitcoin as a wallet does, so we rate it {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosendreceive" type='short' %}. Like every product in this category, it is also custodial.
