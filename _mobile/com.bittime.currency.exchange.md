## Android

## App Description

Bittime is a centralized Indonesian crypto-asset exchange registered with BAPPEBTI and operating under PT Utama Aset Digital Indonesia, as detailed in its official [User Agreement](https://support.bittime.com/hc/en-us/articles/5786767795343-User-Agreement
). The document outlines trading rules, fees, KYC/AML requirements, and customer obligations.

## Analysis

The app is geo-restricted and only available in Indonesian. We were not able to test it. Their terms and conditions is stored as png files. 

The terms describe a sign up process with kyc and ID verification.

After translating each page we did not find any mention of how the assets are stored or how the private keys are managed by the platform. 

This [statement](https://support.bittime.com/hc/en-us/articles/5786767795343-User-Agreement) stands out though: 

(translated using Google translate)

> Customers who do not perform KYC verification cannot make withdrawals.

This is a **custodial** service.
