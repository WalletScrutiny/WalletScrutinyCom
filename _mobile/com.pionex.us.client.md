## App Description

PionexUS provides access to cryptocurrency trading where users can buy, sell, and automate trades using exchange-provided trading bots, as described in the Play Store listing. It supports Bitcoin among other assets, but operates strictly as a custodial trading platform rather than a self-custodial wallet, since all assets remain under the platform’s control. The app allows spot trading, automated strategies, and account management functions tied to Pionex’s centralized exchange infrastructure.
