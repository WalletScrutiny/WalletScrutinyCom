**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its [archived store page](http://web.archive.org/web/20201111214122/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.hibana.hibana) from November 2020 described a Lightning wallet with automatic channel opening, Submarine Swaps and a browser for Lightning apps, with a developer address at data-tower.com and version 0.1 from December 2018. data-tower.com no longer resolves and www.hibana.app points to a Namecheap parking page. Neither source says who holds the keys, so it stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %}.

**Update 2021-11-17**: This app is not on Play Store anymore.
