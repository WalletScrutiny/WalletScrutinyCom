**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play, but an [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230925211845/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=mw.mwc.wallet) shows a wallet that can "securely store, send and receive MimbleWimbleCoin (MWC)". Bitcoin appears there only as the other side of peer-to-peer atomic swaps, not as a coin the wallet holds, so we now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nobtc" type='short' %}. The [MWC project](https://www.mwc.mw/) is still online. MWC is also supported by the multi-coin {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.cypherstack.stackwallet' verdict='true' %}.
