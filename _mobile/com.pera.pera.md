## App Description

Pera is a Nigerian app for trading crypto and gift cards, paying bills (airtime, data, electricity, cable TV) and shopping in the "Pera Shop". Its website, [perahq.com](https://perahq.com/), names the operator as Pera Technologies LTD (RC:7897661). The Play description promises to "Buy and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Shiba Inu, Solana, Cardano, and more" and says "Your assets are protected by SAFC (Secure Asset Fund for Clients)". The app replaces the earlier Pera app, {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.ewebmeta.ewebmeta' verdict='true' %}. Both list pera.cx as their website and use the same support address, support@pera.cx, and Pera's [support page](https://perahq.com/support.html) still links to the "eweb-meta" LinkedIn page. The pera.cx domain no longer resolves.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play description lists "Bitcoin (BTC)" first among the "100+ cryptocurrencies" users can trade.
- **Sending and receiving.** The Play description says: "Send and receive crypto with just a few taps. Use wallet addresses or QR codes for fast and secure transfers." The [terms](https://perahq.com/terms.html) say the wallet lets users instruct Pera "to send Digital Currency to, or receive and store it from, third parties".
- **Custody.** The [terms](https://perahq.com/terms.html) describe the service as "Hosted digital currency wallets that allow you to store, monitor, transfer, and oversee the balances of specific supported digital currencies such as BITCOIN, ETHEREUM, BNB, and Doge". They also cap Pera's liability at "the combined value of Digital Currency in your Digital Currency Wallet". The terms mention no seed phrase or user-held keys. Their claim that the service lacks "regulation from the Financial Conduct Authority" points to text copied from a UK template. Our review of the earlier app found no way to back up the keys either.

Pera holds the coins in hosted wallets, so this app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, like the app it replaces.

## iPhone

The iOS app is published under the developer name PeraHQ instead of Project360 Software. It is the same Pera service with the same version number and the same account, so the same verdict applies.
