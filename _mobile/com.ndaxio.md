## App Description

Ndax is a Canadian crypto trading platform run by NDAX Inc. The app buys, sells and stakes over 60 crypto assets, Bitcoin among them, with CAD funding through Interac e-Transfer and wire transfer. The Play description mentions "Deposit crypto directly into your account". It adds that Canadian Investor Protection Fund coverage "applies to fiat currency held in your account" and that "CIPF does not protect virtual assets".

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** Ndax's announcement [New Ndax Bitcoin Withdrawal Options and Fee Schedule](https://ndax.io/en/blog/article/new-ndax-bitcoin-withdrawal-options-and-fee-schedule) describes "new enhanced Bitcoin withdrawal options designed to provide you with more choice, flexibility, and transparency when moving your Bitcoin into self-custody". It lists Express (0.000299 BTC), Standard (0.000149 BTC) and free "Flex" withdrawals. The Play description confirms crypto deposits.
- **Custody.** Ndax's [Security and Compliance page](https://ndax.io/en/security) states: "A majority of client digital assets are held with acceptable third-party custodians, with at least 80% of client crypto assets held in segregated omnibus accounts in the name of Ndax in trust for or for the benefit of Ndax's clients". It adds: "Ndax uses a combination of cold, warm, and hot wallet infrastructure to support asset custody, trading, deposits, withdrawals, and settlement". Client assets used for staking "remain in the possession, custody, and control of Ndax at all times".

Ndax and its custodians hold the keys to client bitcoin in accounts in Ndax's name, so the app is [custodial](/methodology/#custodial).

## iOS

There is also an [iOS version on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id1412219882). It is published by NDAX INC, the same provider, and it gives access to the same account and service, so the same verdict applies to it.
