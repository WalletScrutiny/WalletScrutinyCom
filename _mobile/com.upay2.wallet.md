## App Description

UPay is a digital finance platform that combines cryptocurrency services with traditional financial tools in a single mobile app.

At its core is the UPay Web3 Wallet, which supports major cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and USDC, alongside features for storage, transfers, and payments.

The app offers crypto-linked virtual and physical cards, crypto-to-fiat conversion, multi-currency support, and integration with Apple Pay and Google Pay for everyday spending.

UPay positions itself as an all-in-one fintech solution providing wallet services, payments, remittances, and yield-generating crypto finance products within a unified ecosystem.
