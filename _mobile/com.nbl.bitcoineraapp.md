**Update 2026-10-30:** This app is still gone from Google Play. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20201111145545/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nbl.bitcoineraapp) from November 2020 names the developer Nebel Infotech and describes an "automated system" for crypto trading whose makers earn "a small commission from getting you started with a brokerage". The page names no website and does not say whether the app itself holds any coins or only signs users up with a broker, so it stays a work in progress.

**Update 2021-08-12**: This app is not on the Play Store anymore
