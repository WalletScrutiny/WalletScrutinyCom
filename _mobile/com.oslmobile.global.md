## App Description

OSL is a cryptocurrency trading platform operated by BC Technology (HK) Limited, Hong Kong's first SFC-licensed digital asset platform (Stock Code: 863.HK). The app provides fiat-to-crypto on/off-ramp services supporting USD deposits via SWIFT or local bank transfers. Users can trade Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other cryptocurrencies through the platform's Flash Trade and Pro Trade interfaces. The app implements two-factor authentication (2FA), biometric login, and claims to use cold wallet custody for asset storage, with institutional-grade security protocols and insurance coverage.

## Analysis

The claimed use of cold wallet custody gives us an insight to the **custodial** nature of this app.
