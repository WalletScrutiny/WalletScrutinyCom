**Update 2026-10-30:** We now file this app as {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The [archived store page](http://web.archive.org/web/20220303013457/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitforex.pro) describes BitForex Pro as the app of the BitForex exchange, the same exchange as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.bitforex.bitforex' verdict='true' %}. In February 2024 BitForex went offline after large outflows from its hot wallets, and [bitforex.com](https://www.bitforex.com/) is now an "Asset Service Center" that posts monthly liquidation notices.

**Update 2022-03-26**: This app is not available anymore.
