## App Description

FLOQ is an Indonesian crypto exchange app operated by PT Kripto Maksima Koin. The Play Store description calls it "a licensed digital asset exchange, directly supervised by Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK)". Users deposit rupiah via virtual account, GoPay, QRIS or other e-wallets, then trade Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, Solana and "dozens of other popular crypto assets". FLOQ's consumer agreement says customer crypto is kept in the company's wallet.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** FLOQ's [list of coins and tokens](https://help.floq.id/hc/id-id/articles/13362279999375-Daftar-Koin-dan-Token-yang-Tersedia-di-FLOQ) lists BTC on the "Bitcoin" network, with a transfer fee of 0.00004 BTC and a minimum withdrawal of 0.0001 BTC. Another help article confirms "Bitcoin menggunakan jaringan Bitcoin" ("Bitcoin uses the Bitcoin network") for deposits and withdrawals ([source](https://help.floq.id/hc/id-id/articles/13303883958927-Apakah-Setiap-Aset-Kripto-Menggunakan-Jaringan-yang-Sama)).
- **Sending and receiving.** The [transfer guide](https://help.floq.id/hc/id-id/articles/12306548004879-Panduan-Transfer-Aset-Kripto-di-FLOQ) describes withdrawals: choose "Transfer", choose the asset, enter "alamat wallet tujuan" (the destination wallet address), pick the network and confirm with OTP/2FA. For deposits, FLOQ says it "tidak mengenakan biaya deposit crypto" ("does not charge a crypto deposit fee") for crypto sent "dari wallet atau exchange lain" ("from another wallet or exchange") ([source](https://help.floq.id/hc/id-id/articles/15937068837391-Memahami-Biaya-Deposit-Crypto-Fee-Transfer)).
- **Custody.** The [consumer agreement](https://floq.co.id/perjanjiankonsumen) says that for a delisted asset the company may move "Aset Kripto tertentu milik Konsumen yang disimpan pada wallet milik Perusahaan kepada wallet milik Konsumen" ("certain crypto assets of the Consumer that are stored in the Company's wallet to the Consumer's wallet"). It also lets the company "mendebet Aset Kripto pada dompet digital Platform" ("debit crypto assets in the Platform's digital wallet") to cover its losses from suspicious transactions. The [terms and conditions](https://floq.co.id/syaratdanketentuan) add that "Floq berhak untuk menutup Akun dan membekukan dana, Aset Kripto, dan transaksi" ("Floq has the right to close the account and freeze funds, crypto assets and transactions").

By its own agreement, FLOQ keeps customers' crypto in wallets the company owns and can debit or freeze it. It is [custodial](/methodology/#custodial).
