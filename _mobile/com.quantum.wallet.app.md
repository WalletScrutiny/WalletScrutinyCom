## Android

## App Description

Google Play describes Quantum Wallet as a self-custody, multi-chain crypto wallet that doubles as a gateway to Web3 dApps. The listing states that private keys remain encrypted on the user’s device, the app doesn’t collect contact or personal details, and WalletConnect support is built in. It further claims support for millions of assets across five blockchains (including BTC, ETH, SOL, BCH, and MATIC) plus NFTs, with in-app buy/sell, swap, stake, and third-party fiat on-ramps, and notes a crypto debit card integration planned for Q3 2024.
