## App Description

Maxbit is a Bitcoin and cryptocurrency trading platform in Thailand regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). The platform supports digital assets including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Ripple (XRP), USDT, and BTZ. Maxbit provides a digital wallet designed for managing digital assets with support for multiple coins. The platform features Thai Baht deposit integration, ISO 27001 security measures, and 24/7 customer service via live chat.
