## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still custodial. In February 2025 attackers took [almost $1.5 billion from one of Bybit's Ethereum multisig cold wallets](https://www.bybit.com/en/learn/this-week-in-bybit/bybit-security-incident-timeline), the same kind of "multi-signature cold-wallet solution" the description quoted below presents as protection. Bybit said it was solvent and could cover the loss. The app also contains a separate Web3 "Seed Phrase Wallet" that Bybit [describes as noncustodial](https://www.bybit.com/en/learn/web3/bybit-web3-wallets-comparison) and that [can be created within the Bybit App](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Seed-Phrase-Wallet). Its Cloud Wallet and Keyless Wallet [were shut down on May 31, 2025](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-Discontinuation-of-Restricted-Product-Offerings-on-Wallet). We found no Bybit page confirming that the Seed Phrase Wallet holds native Bitcoin, so the exchange account, which is where this app's Bitcoin sits, keeps the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

> "Bybit is the World's fastest-growing and one of the largest crypto
  derivatives exchanges to trade Bitcoin and crypto.

and as such, funds are in cold storage with them:

> YOUR SAFETY IS OUR PRIORITY<br>
  We safeguard your cryptocurrencies with a multi-signature cold-wallet
  solution. Your funds are 100% protected from the prying eyes. All traders'
  deposited assets are segregated from Bybit's operating budget to increase our
  financial accountability and transparency.

As a custodial app it is **not verifiable**.
