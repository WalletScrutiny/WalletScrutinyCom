**⚠️ Warning:** The provider can no longer be reached: its website, X accounts and email addresses are gone, and a Google Play review from September 2026 reports BTC that never showed up in the app. Do not send coins to this app.

## App Description

W3 Wallet is a multichain DeFi wallet published by W3 Wallet Tech. The Play Store description calls it "Your self-custodial DeFi super-wallet" and says "your private keys never leave your device". Under "SUPPORTED NETWORKS" it lists Bitcoin first, followed by Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain, Algorand, Solana, Tron and TON. Features include swaps, lending through Aave, Folks Finance, Compound and Morpho, yield vaults and quests with token rewards.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** We rely on the Play Store description, which lists "Bitcoin" among the supported networks. No other source was available to confirm it (see below).
- **Custody.** The description claims the wallet is "Truly self-custodial – your private keys never leave your device". We found no terms or help pages from the provider that say otherwise, or that say anything at all.
- **Who was behind it.** The Play developer "W3 Wallet Tech" is registered as LAPTEV-FZCO, a Dubai Silicon Oasis free-zone company, and the archived [contacts page](https://web.archive.org/web/20250907144357/https://w3wallet.app/en/contacts) gives its licence number (46365). Its CTO, Roman Laptev, [lists the role on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rolaman/). The project was small but real: the website was translated into eight languages, it ran a [Galxe quest](https://app.galxe.com/quest/W3Wallet), and in September 2025 its [Telegram channel](https://t.me/s/w3wallet_app) announced an Algorand swap competition with a 4,000 ALGO prize pool. We found no funding, audits or press coverage.
- **The provider is gone.** The website [w3wallet.app](https://w3wallet.app) now shows a GoDaddy placeholder page. The archived homepage linked an X account, `w3wallet_app`, which was active from October 2024 to at least August 2025 and has since been deleted, as has an earlier account, `w3_wallet`, and the LinkedIn company page. Neither w3wallet.app nor w3wallet.com can receive email (no MX record), and the listed privacy policy, `https://api.w3wallet.app/policy`, did not respond. A Google Play review from 2026-09-17 says: "I transfered my BTC to this app but did reflect… emails bounced". An [archived copy of the website from May 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250512141456/https://www.w3wallet.app/) described a "New Non-Custodial Multichain Wallet" to "Send, receive, and store tokens from 8+ blockchains", but said nothing about keys or source code. The app was last updated on Google Play on 2025-10-21, and the Telegram channel's last post is from 2025-10-01. The iPhone version, {% include walletLink.html wallet='iphone/app.w3wallet.wallet' verdict='true' %}, is already listed on WalletScrutiny and has been removed from the App Store.
- **Source code.** The archived website does not link to any code repository. A GitHub repository search for "w3wallet" returned only unrelated hobby projects. A GitHub code search for `app.w3wallet` found only third-party wallet lists and our own listing. A GitHub organization called [w3wallet](https://github.com/w3wallet) has only one repository, "Token assets for w3wallet", last updated in 2023. It has no app code, and we could not tell whether it belongs to this provider.

We found no public source code for this app, so the provider's claim of self-custody cannot be verified. The app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosource" type='short' %}. The provider stopped operating around October 2025, so we also mark the app {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="defunct" type='short' %}.
