**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20190923143528/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.uun.clet) from September 2019 described an airdrop and ICO portal: airdropped tokens "pile into 'My Wallet'", you invest in ICOs with BTC and ETH, and you withdraw coins from the app to an exchange or a personal wallet. Tokens credited by the provider and withdrawn on request are held by the provider, so the app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The maker, UUNIO, listed Clet among its products on an [archived uun.io page](https://web.archive.org/web/20211022095642/https://uun.io/); uun.io itself no longer loads (Cloudflare error 530), and clet.io now redirects to an unrelated gambling site.

**Update 2022-01-15**: This app is not on the Store anymore.
