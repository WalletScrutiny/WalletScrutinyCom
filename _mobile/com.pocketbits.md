**Update 2026-10-30:** In May 2026 PocketBits posted a notice on its website that Ad Astra Labs had taken over PocketBits, Zelta and Blockend, apologised to users waiting on their funds, and said the platforms were being merged ([archived notice](https://web.archive.org/web/20260505201337/https://www.pocketbits.in/)). The website now only shows a bare trading terminal, and the app has not been updated since 2023.

## App Description

## The App

We tried the app and registered an account. After signing in, we were taken to the app's dashboard. There are tabs listing Trade, Vault, My Portfolio, and Wallet. Users are asked to complete KYC to unlock all features. 

No mnemonics are provided to users.

## Verdict

With the information we have collected above, we conclude that PocketBits is a **custodial** exchange. It is thus **not verifiable.**
