**Update 2026-10-30:** This app was not a Bitcoin wallet. An [archived store page from September 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210926174610/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.liquiditynetwork.wallet) offers to "spend DAI, ETH, LQD and more coins", all of them on Ethereum, and the app's [source code](https://github.com/liquidity-network/mobile-wallet) depends on Ethereum libraries only. We now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nobtc" type='short' %}.

**Update 2022-02-28**: This app is not available anymore.
