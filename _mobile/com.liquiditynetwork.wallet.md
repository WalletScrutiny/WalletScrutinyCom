**Update 2026-10-30:** This app was not a Bitcoin wallet. An [archived store page from September 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210926174610/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.liquiditynetwork.wallet) offers a "self-custodial account" to "spend DAI, ETH, LQD and more coins" and to hold and swap three currencies, all of them on Ethereum. The provider published the app's [source code](https://github.com/liquidity-network/mobile-wallet), a React Native wallet for its NOCUST Ethereum layer 2. It uses the same app ID and depends on Ethereum libraries only. The app is still gone from Google Play, and liquidity.network no longer resolves. We now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nobtc" type='short' %}.

**Update 2022-02-28**: This app is not available anymore.
