## App Description

Bitcointry is an Android trading app that allows users to buy and sell digital assets with real-time market data and instant trade execution. The Play Store listing claims both “full custodial control” and “your own keys,” though these statements conflict technically and require verification.

## Analysis

From its [terms: Section 4 "Deposits, Custody, Withdrawals and Transfers"](https://bitcointry.com/en/term)

> All Digital Assets held in your Account are held by us on a custodial basis.

This is a **custodial** app.
