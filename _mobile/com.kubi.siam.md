## App Description

KuCoin Thailand was the app of ERX Co., Ltd., a licensed Thai exchange operating under the KuCoin brand (developer name "KCTH"). It traded Bitcoin, Ethereum and other crypto against the Thai baht. The app has not been updated since October 2025, and the service is closing: KuCoin Thailand says it is "in the process of returning the Digital Asset Exchange License to the Ministry of Finance". KuCoin's global app is listed separately as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.kubi.kucoin' verdict='true' %}.

## Analysis

- **Service status.** The [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/digital-assets/40060709) reports that the Thai SEC "has ordered a temporary suspension of business operations at ERX Co., Ltd. (KuCoin Thailand)" after its capital fell below the minimum. KuCoin Thailand's announcement [Important Announcement: Asset Withdrawal, Temporary System Suspension, and Custody Fee Policy](https://www.kucoin.th/en/announcement/20254-35) asked customers to withdraw all assets "by April 22, 2026, at 13:00 (UTC+7)". After that, "Customers will not be able to log in" and "Customers will not be able to withdraw assets independently. Manual withdrawal requests must be submitted to Customer Service". The later announcement [Return of Digital Asset Exchange License](https://www.kucoin.th/en/announcement/en-return-of-digital-asset-exchange-license) confirms that withdrawals now go through `happy@kucoin.th`.
- **Bitcoin support.** The Play description says the app "makes it easy to buy and sell Bitcoin (BTC)", and [kucoin.th](https://www.kucoin.th/en/privilege) still lists a BTC/THB market. The withdrawal announcement told users to "Transfer digital assets to your own wallet (e.g., hardware wallet or software wallet where you hold the private key)". It warned that "Deposits made to KuCoin Thailand's existing wallet addresses after April 22, 2026 ... will not be recoverable". So crypto deposits and withdrawals existed, but deposits are now closed. We could not read the old help centre: kucoin-th.zendesk.com shows "Help Center Closed".
- **Custody.** The same announcement introduces a "monthly custody fee for customer assets" from May 2026. It also says "After digital assets are transferred out of the Company's system, the Company will no longer have control, oversight, or access to such assets". The company controls the assets until they are withdrawn.
- **History.** KuCoin, whose brand this Thai exchange used, lost about $281M from its hot wallets in a September 2020 hack and recovered most of it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KuCoin)). In January 2025 its operator Peken Global pleaded guilty in the US to running an unlicensed money transmitting business, agreed to pay over $297M and to leave the US market ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2025/01/28/kucoin-hit-with-nearly-usd300-million-fine-after-pleading-guilty-to-u-s-doj-charges)). The main KuCoin app is listed as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.kubi.kucoin' verdict='true' %}.

KuCoin Thailand holds its customers' bitcoin and charges a custody fee on what remains, so the app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The service no longer accepts deposits or logins, so the listing may be better marked as defunct.
