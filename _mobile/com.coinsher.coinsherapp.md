## App Description

Coinsher is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange registered in Seychelles (Coinsher Global
Limited). It offers spot trading, P2P, crypto swaps, and a buy-crypto fiat on-ramp. The
platform requires account creation with KYC verification.

## Analysis

Coinsher is a **custodial** exchange. The platform offers spot trading which requires users
to deposit funds into platform-controlled accounts. Their Terms of Use state:

> "We will not be responsible for any losses that you incur as a result of trading on our platform."

The product lineup — spot, P2P, swap — all operate on platform-held balances. Their own
blog recommends moving long-term holdings to personal hardware wallets while using Coinsher
for trading, confirming that funds are held by the platform during trading. There is no
mention of private keys anywhere in the terms or app description. Investigation stops here.
