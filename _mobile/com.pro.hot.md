**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is back on Google Play and Hotcoin is an active
exchange with a working [website](https://www.hotcoin.com/en_US/), so it is not
defunct. Hotcoin says it keeps
[reserve assets of over $137 million](https://www.hotcoin.com/en_US/learn/article/Hotcoin-updates-PoR/)
to back its users' balances, with public wallet addresses for verification. An
exchange that holds reserves against user balances holds the keys, so the app is
{% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The app
description also mentions Web3 wallet features. We did not review those; as with
other exchanges that offer an optional in-app wallet, the verdict is custodial.
