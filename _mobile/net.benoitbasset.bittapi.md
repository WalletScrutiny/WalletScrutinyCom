**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is gone from Google Play; the Internet Archive still saw its [store page in July 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250708030042/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.benoitbasset.bittapi). It was an unofficial client for the Bittrex exchange to check balances and place orders, as the [developer's page](http://bittapi.oblade.net/) still says. Bittrex Global announced in November 2023 that it would stop all trading by December 4 ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/206761/crypto-bittrex-global-shuts-down-sec)), so the app has nothing left to connect to. Neither page mentions deposits or withdrawals, and we cannot test a removed app, so it stays a work in progress.
