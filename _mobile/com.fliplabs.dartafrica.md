## App Description

DartAfrica is a cryptocurrency exchange platform operating in Ghana and Nigeria that enables users to sell and trade Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, BUSD, and other cryptocurrencies for local fiat currency (NGN/GHS).

The platform markets itself as a "crypto trading platform" with features including "lightning-fast transactions," "cold storage of funds," and 24/7 customer support.

## Analysis

Their website is currently unavailable. 

DartAfrica positions itself as a cryptocurrency **exchange** rather than a self-custodial wallet. The app description focuses entirely on **trading, selling, and exchanging** cryptocurrencies, with no mention of:
- Private key control
- Seed phrases or recovery phrases
- Sending/receiving to external Bitcoin addresses
- Self-custody features

The mention of "cold storage of funds" and "fund transfer pin" indicates that DartAfrica holds user funds on their platform, making this a **custodial** service. Users do not control the private keys to their Bitcoin or other cryptocurrency holdings.

This is a cryptocurrency exchange, not a Bitcoin wallet. By WalletScrutiny's standards, this is **custodial**.
