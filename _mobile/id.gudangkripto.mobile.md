## App Description

GudangKripto is the app of PT Gudang Kripto Indonesia, which the Play Store description calls "a Digital Financial Asset innovation company focused on crypto assets, acting as an intermediary between buyers and sellers of crypto assets". The platform is "designed for large-scale centralized crypto asset trading". It says it is registered with "CFX as a Crypto Exchange, KKI as the Clearing Institution, and ICC as the Custodian Wallet Institution", and supervised by OJK. Its market page lists BTC/IDR.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** GudangKripto's [market page](https://gudangkripto.id/market) lists "BTC/IDR BITCOIN". Its [trading procedures](https://gudangkripto.id/legal/trading-procedures) give "Bitcoin (BTC)" as an example of assets to buy.
- **Sending and receiving.** The trading procedures include a "Guide to Crypto Asset Deposits": select the crypto asset "(e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum, or USDT)", "Copy the Wallet Address" and send from "the application or Crypto Asset wallet where your assets are stored". The "Guide to Crypto Asset Withdrawals" has the user select the asset "(e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum, or USDT)" and enter a "Destination Wallet Address", after which "the Crypto Assets will be transferred to the destination wallet". The [homepage](https://gudangkripto.id/) FAQ says: "You can withdraw crypto assets to an external wallet, another exchange, or fellow GudangKripto users via Funding Wallet".
- **Custody.** The Play Store description names "ICC as the Custodian Wallet Institution". The homepage says: "Your assets are safely encrypted and stored offline." The [terms](https://gudangkripto.id/legal/terms) define the deposit address as "the Member's Digital Asset wallet address ... usable repeatedly as a Digital Asset wallet provided on the platform". They also state that "GudangKripto has the right to close the Account and freeze the funds, Digital Assets, and transactions therein if suspicious activity is found", and that the member "authorizes GudangKripto to debit Digital Assets in the GudangKripto digital wallet" to cover the company's losses.

GudangKripto and its custodian hold users' crypto in wallets the platform can freeze and debit, so it is [custodial](/methodology/#custodial).
