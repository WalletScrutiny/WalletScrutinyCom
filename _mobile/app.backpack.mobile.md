## App Description 

Backpack is a multi-chain crypto platform that combines a self-custodial wallet with a regulated exchange. Users can trade spot, margin, and futures markets from a unified cross-margined account, earn yield on collateral, and claim trading rewards through its leveling system. The exchange portion supports Bitcoin (BTC) deposits and trading pairs, alongside other major assets, providing a custodial environment for executing trades under compliance oversight.

The self-custodial Backpack Wallet, however, focuses on Solana, Ethereum, Eclipse, Monad, and similar ecosystems, enabling users to create and manage wallets, swap or bridge tokens, and interact with NFTs and decentralized apps. Native Bitcoin (BTC) is [not supported](https://support.backpack.exchange/wallet/what-is-backpack-wallet) in this self-custody mode. This distinction means Backpack users can trade BTC on the exchange but cannot hold or transact native Bitcoin directly within the non-custodial wallet. 

## Analysis

It is possible to [deposit BTC](https://backpack.exchange/market-info/assets) (on the bitcoin blockchain) if the user chooses to sign-up to its custodial service. 

Quoting from its [End User Agreement](https://support.backpack.exchange/legal/general-legal/user-agreement): 

> 9.3 Virtual Asset Wallet. Your Virtual Asset Wallet allows you to store, track, transfer, and manage your balances of Supported Virtual Assets. We securely store Virtual Asset private keys, which are used to process transactions, in a combination of online and offline storage.

The exchange portion of the app is **custodial** while the wallet **does not have BTC support**.
