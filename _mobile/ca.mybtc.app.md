## App Description

MyBTC.ca is a Canadian cryptocurrency broker, registered as a Money Service Business with FINTRAC according to its [FAQ](https://mybtc.ca/faq). The app lets users fund an account in Canadian dollars by Interac e-Transfer, bank wire, card or Flexepin voucher, then buy Bitcoin, Ether or USD Coin. Users can also sell these coins for CAD. The website calls the service a "Non Custodial Cryptocurrency Exchange" and says it sends purchased coins to the user's own wallet.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** Bitcoin can be bought and sold through the app. The description says users can "buy & sell anywhere from $20 to $500,000 worth of Bitcoin or Ether".
- **No crypto balance.** The [FAQ](https://mybtc.ca/faq) says: "Unlike custodial exchanges, MyBTC.ca does not store cryptocurrency on behalf of customers, so you'll need your own compatible wallet to receive your purchases." It also says: "MyBTC.ca sends your purchased cryptocurrency directly to your designated wallet address". When buying, the user enters a "destination wallet address" with the order.
- **Selling.** To sell, the user sends coins from an external wallet "to the designated receiving wallet address provided". After confirmation, the coins are "converted immediately to CAD", which can be withdrawn by Interac e-Transfer or bank transfer. The account balance therefore holds only Canadian dollars.
- The [home page](https://mybtc.ca/) says that MyBTC.ca will "mandate you, as our valued customer, to send your purchased Bitcoin and Ether to an external wallet".

The app buys bitcoin into a wallet the user brings and keeps no bitcoin balance itself. It is a broker, not a wallet, as described in [our methodology](/methodology/#nowallet).
