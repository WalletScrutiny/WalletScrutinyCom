## App Description

YUBIT is a centralized cryptocurrency exchange that supports Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies through spot trading, futures contracts, and copy-trading features. User funds are held custodially by YUBIT using hot and cold wallet infrastructure, with trading activity executed entirely within the platform rather than via user-controlled on-chain wallets.
