**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play, but the Internet Archive kept its [store page from May 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210517133513/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fluidcoins.fluidcoins). It was a Nigerian app to buy, sell and auto-invest in Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT, with fiat withdrawals to a Nigerian bank account, and it told users to "secure your cryptocurrencies in our secure wallet". Fluidcoins held the coins, so we now list it as {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The company was [acquired by Blockfinex](https://techpoint.africa/2023/02/16/blockfinex-acquires-fluidcoins/) in February 2023, and [fluidcoins.com](https://fluidcoins.com/) now sells crypto payment infrastructure to African businesses.

**Update 2021-11-16**: This app is not on Play Store anymore.
