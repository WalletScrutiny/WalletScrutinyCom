## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** WazirX is still a custodial exchange, and its custody
failed once. On 2024-07-18 it lost about US$235 million in a hack and froze
trading and withdrawals
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WazirX_hack)). Singapore's High
Court approved the restructuring scheme of its operator Zettai on 2025-10-13
([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/10/13/wazirx-restructuring-cleared-in-massive-relief-for-usd230m-hack-victims)),
and the exchange restarted on 2025-10-24, returning about 85% of claims with the
rest in recovery tokens ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_WazirX_hack)).
The verdict stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

**Update 2021-08-09**: As this exchange allows holding your BTC in the app such
as sending and receiving them, it is usable as a wallet. A custodial wallet. As
such it is **not verifiable**.

This mobile app is an exchange based trading solution and is not a wallet.
