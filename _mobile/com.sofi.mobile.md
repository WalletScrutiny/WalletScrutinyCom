## Android

## App Description

SoFi is a mobile financial app that combines banking, investing, lending, and personal finance tools into a single platform.

The app supports buying, selling, and holding Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, but crypto assets are managed through SoFi’s custodial trading service rather than a self-custodied wallet.

Users can access checking and savings accounts, stock and ETF investing, credit cards, loans, and budgeting features within the same app.

SoFi positions itself as an all-in-one digital finance hub aimed at simplifying everyday money management through an integrated mobile experience.

## Analysis

It is possible to [deposit Bitcoin to a hosted account](https://support.sofi.com/hc/en-us/articles/41380634094861-How-do-I-transfer-in-crypto-from-an-external-wallet).

But it is [not possible to transfer Bitcoin](https://support.sofi.com/hc/en-us/articles/41382707894669-Am-I-able-to-withdraw-my-crypto-to-an-external-wallet) to an external wallet.

SoFi support explains that while you can receive certain cryptocurrencies (including Bitcoin) into your SoFi Crypto account from an external wallet by using the app’s unique deposit address, there is no functionality to withdraw crypto out to an external wallet.

So, not only is this app a custodial one, but **can only receive, and not send**.
