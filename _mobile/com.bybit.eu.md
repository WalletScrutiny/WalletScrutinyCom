## App Description

Bybit EU is a centralized, MiCAR-regulated cryptocurrency trading application operated by Bybit EU GmbH, providing EU users access to spot trading and fiat on-ramps for assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and Solana.

All Bitcoin and crypto balances are held in segregated custodial accounts under EU safeguarding rules, meaning users do not control private keys and all custody operations are performed by Bybit’s regulated infrastructure.

The app supports KYC-required features including spot trading, One-Click Buy, fiat deposits via cards or SEPA, and EU-compliant yield products such as Rewards Service and Launchpool.

Because the platform performs custody, trading, staking, and asset management centrally, Bybit EU functions as a custodial Bitcoin service rather than a self-custodial wallet.
