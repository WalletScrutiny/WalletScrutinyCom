## App Description

BIT.EU is a centralized cryptocurrency trading platform that provides account-based access to spot markets, futures pairs, and automated copy-trading features. The app supports deposits, withdrawals, and trading across hundreds of assets, including BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, and numerous altcoins, with fiat on-ramps for multiple global currencies. All custody, security controls, and KYC processes are handled by the exchange, which advertises features such as 2FA, in-app passcodes, and rapid verification.

## Analysis

> The commitment to constant scrutiny is reinforced through regular public disclosures of reserve ratios for BTC, ETH, USDT, and USDC. The last snapshot revealed platform assets surpass user assets by significant margins, with BTC holdings of over 28,000 BTC against user BTC liabilities of approximately 7,680 BTC. This always-verified asset sufficiency, combined with the publishing of Merkle tree root hashes representing over 41 million records in the snapshot, guarantees that the platform's integrity is continuously monitored and available for user verification. Moreover, public wallet balance disclosures showing allocations such as 6,500 BTC held across multiple cold wallets provide an additional layer of transparency and continuous external validation.

The app operates entirely through BIT.EU’s **custodial** infrastructure.
