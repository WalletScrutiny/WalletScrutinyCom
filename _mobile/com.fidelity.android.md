## App Description

Fidelity is a unified brokerage application offering trading, account management, portfolio analysis, and cash-movement tools across multiple asset classes.

The app supports Bitcoin through Fidelity Crypto®, enabling BTC trading, transfers, and custody within a regulated digital-asset account.

Its trading interface provides market data, order execution tools, alerts, and integrated research for both traditional securities and supported cryptocurrencies.

Crypto functionality is operated by Fidelity Digital Asset Services, a NYDFS-regulated trust, which handles digital-asset custody and blockchain transaction processing.

## Analysis

Fidelity Crypto has an entire [section](https://www.fidelity.com/crypto/trading) on how to buy, sell and transfer Bitcoin.

The [video](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/crypto/how-to-use-fid-crypto-fullep2025-vid) discusses in length that users can transfer Bitcoin to an external address. 

This is a **custodial** service as noted in the [footer of this fidelity page.](https://newsroom.fidelity.com/pressreleases)

> Custody and trading of crypto are provided by Fidelity Digital Asset Services, LLC, a New York State-chartered limited liability trust company (NMLS ID 1773897).
