## Android

Webull Pay and Webull Trade are separate apps with different regulatory scopes, even though both are operated by Webull.

{% include walletLink.html wallet='android/org.dayup.stocks' verdict='true' %} is the primary brokerage app for stocks, ETFs, and options; crypto exposure there is limited and framed as part of a broader trading platform.

**Webull Pay** (this app) is a dedicated crypto custody and payments app, governed by its own crypto-specific terms, where digital assets are held in omnibus accounts controlled by Webull Pay or its service providers.

## App Description

This app provides a crytocurrency trading platform that allows users to buy/sell BTC, LTC, ETH, DOGE, SHIB and other cryptocurrencies. 

It claims: 

> Cryptocurrency execution services are provided by Bakkt Crypto Solutions, LLC and Bakkt Marketplace, LLC (“Bakkt”, NMLS ID 1828849/1890144) through a software licensing agreement between Bakkt and Webull Pay. Bakkt is not a registered broker-dealer or a member of SIPC or FINRA, and your crypto balance is not FDIC or SIPC insured. Bakkt Crypto Solutions, LLC and Bakkt Marketplace, LLC are licensed to engage in virtual currency business activity by the New York State Department of Financial Services.

## Analysis

In their ["Virtual Currency User Agreement"](https://www.webullpay.com/protocol/virtual-currency-user-agreement), they describe how users funds are handled:

> 5) Omnibus Accounts. To securely and efficiently custody assets, Webull Pay may use shared blockchain addresses (controlled by Webull Pay or its Service Providers) to hold Supported Digital Assets for customers. These omnibus blockchain addresses may pool customer assets with those of other users and, in some cases, with assets held by Webull Pay for its own benefit. While we maintain separate internal records and ledgers to track your ownership of specific balances, we are not required to assign a dedicated or segregated blockchain address to you. Your Virtual Currency holdings are recorded and maintained on our books and records as being beneficially owned by you, and we treat those assets as separate from our corporate assets.

This is a **custodial** service.
