## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still custodial, and a 2026 incident shows it plainly. In February 2026 Bithumb [credited 620,000 bitcoins to 695 customers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/07/south-korean-crypto-firm-accidentally-sends-out-44-billion-in-bitcoin.html) in a promotion that was meant to pay out 2,000 won each. Bithumb restricted trading and withdrawals for the affected accounts within 35 minutes and said it recovered 99.7% of them. An exchange that can credit and claw back bitcoins at will keeps the balances in its own books, not in wallets the user controls. The verdict stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
