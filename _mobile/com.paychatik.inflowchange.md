## App Description

InflowBit is published by Paychatik Ltd. It is an exchange where users in Nigeria and Ukraine, and PayPal users elsewhere, sell cryptocurrencies for Naira, Hryvnia or PayPal dollars, and buy crypto with cards or bank transfer. The description says each user has a "Cash Wallet" and a "Crypto Wallet" where "you can deposit over 100+ cryptocurrencies and exchange to cash anytime you want". Bitcoin is listed first among the supported coins.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** BTC can be deposited into the in-app "Crypto Wallet" and sold for fiat.
- **No sending.** The FAQ on [inflowbit.com](https://inflowbit.com) asks "Can I send and receive crypto with my InflowBit crypto wallet?" and answers: "No, you can't. Your InflowBit crypto wallet purpose is to only receive or deposit crypto currency in your account to enable you convert/exchange it to cash whenever convenient for you". Payouts go to a "withdrawal method", meaning bank account or PayPal.
- **Custody.** The FAQ says users "deposit crypto to your InflowBit crypto wallet to Instantly convert crypto to cash". The deposited coins sit in an account run by InflowBit, so InflowBit holds them.
- **Same provider, earlier app.** The same developer's earlier app, [Inflowchange](/mobile/com.inflowchange.inflowchange/) (`com.inflowchange.inflowchange`, now removed from Google Play), had an identical FAQ answer and was rated "can't send or receive". InflowBit appears to be the same service under a new name.
- The app has not been updated since August 2024. The inflowbit.com page we read via a rendering proxy shows rates last updated in June 2024, and a direct connection to inflowbit.com failed when we checked.

The provider states that bitcoin deposited into the app can only be converted to fiat, not sent. That matches our ["can't send or receive"](/methodology/#nosendreceive) verdict.
