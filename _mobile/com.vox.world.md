## App Description

VOOX claims to be a global cryptocurrency exchange offering a one-stop trading experience for users to buy and sell digital assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Litecoin. The app claims to support spot trading, USDT-margined perpetual futures, and automated strategy bots that use AI-driven approaches to execute trades. VOOX positions itself as a platform designed to help users access multiple crypto trading products within a single mobile application.

## Analysis

The Google Play page does not list its homepage, although it lists its privacy policy in a similar sounding domain name ("vooxex.com"). However, further searches seem to point to voox.com as its homepage.

If indeed, voox.com is the homepage for this app, we note that it claims:

> VOOX uses secure cold storage solutions to protect your digital assets, minimizing risk and ensuring your funds are safe from unauthorized access

This is a **custodial** service.
