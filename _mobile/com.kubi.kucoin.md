## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** Still custodial. KuCoin [pleaded guilty to US Department of Justice charges in January 2025](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2025/01/28/kucoin-hit-with-nearly-usd300-million-fine-after-pleading-guilty-to-u-s-doj-charges), paid nearly $300 million and agreed to leave the US market for at least two years. The app now also has a "Web3 Wallet" that KuCoin [calls self-custodial](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/en-what-is-the-kucoin-web3-wallet-the-decentralized-tool-that-balances-security-and-convenience), opened from the homepage or assets page of the KuCoin app with a 12-word seed phrase. We found no KuCoin page confirming that it holds native Bitcoin, so the exchange account keeps the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

  securely.

This app is the interface to an exchange. Exchanges are all custodial which
makes the app **not verifiable**.
