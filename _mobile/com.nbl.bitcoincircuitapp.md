**Update 2026-10-30:** This app is still gone from Google Play. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210126101349/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nbl.bitcoincircuitapp) from January 2021 names the developer Nebel Infotech and describes "a computerized application for cryptographic money trading" with a manual and an automated mode. The page names no website and does not say whether the app itself holds any coins or only signs users up with a broker, so it stays a work in progress.

**Update 2021-08-12**: This app is not on the Play Store anymore
