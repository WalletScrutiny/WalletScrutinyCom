## App Description

Firi is a Nordic cryptocurrency exchange registered with Finanstilsynet (Financial Supervisory Authority) in Norway and Denmark, offering Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. The platform provides a "Kryptosparing" savings agreement for recurring cryptocurrency purchases using Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) strategy.

Firi states "we will take care of all your crypto wallets for you" and secures customer funds through partnership with Fireblocks using Multiparty Computation (MPC) technology and cold storage. The platform supports transfers to external wallets, charges 0.7% trading fees, and includes a tax calculator for cryptocurrency investments.

## Analysis

According to their [terms](https://firi.com/about/terms-of-service):

> The company stores the users' cryptocurrencies in digital wallets. A digital wallet for cryptocurrency is software specifically developed to send and receive cryptocurrencies. A wallet contains keys that are used to sign transactions and thus send cryptocurrency.
>
> The company guarantees that the digital wallets are adequately secured. The majority of funds are secured with encryption keys in cold-storage,

This service is **custodial**.
