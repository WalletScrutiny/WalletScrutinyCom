**Update 2026-10-30:** This app is still gone from Google Play. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210624214407/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nbl.bitcoinchampionapp) from June 2021 names the developer Nebel Infotech and describes an automatic "trading robot" for crypto CFDs that needs a deposit of "as little as $250" and relies on partner brokers to handle the money. The page names no website and does not say whether the app itself holds any coins or only signs users up with a broker, so it stays a work in progress.

**Update 2021-08-12**: This app is not on the Play Store anymore
