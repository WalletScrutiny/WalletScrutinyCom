**⚠️ Warning:** This provider is gone. The app is no longer on Google Play and
on 2026-10-30 the domain getvega.app no longer resolved. In 2023 the
[Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20230810114332/https://getvega.app/)
showed only "Future home of Vega" there.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its
[X account](https://x.com/GetVegaApp) still describes the app as "testnet", and
its last post, "Thinking about mainnet.", is from 2020-06-03. An
[archived Play page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210506175117/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.getvega)
from 2021 lists the developer as Mohit Tyagi. We mark the provider as defunct and
keep this listing as a stub.
