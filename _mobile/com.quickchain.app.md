## App Description

QuickChain, published by QUCHANGE TECHNOLOGIES, is a Nigerian app for selling crypto for naira. Users generate deposit addresses for BTC, ETH, USDT, LTC, DOGE and BCH. Deposits are swapped to naira automatically and can be withdrawn to a bank or spent on bills, airtime, betting top-ups and a virtual card.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play description lists "Bitcoin BTC – Convert BTC to Naira in seconds".
- **Receive only, converted on arrival.** The Play description's "How It Works" section reads: "Create a free account in minutes and generate your unique crypto addresses. Deposit your crypto ... It is automatically swapped to naira and deposited to your naira wallet. Withdraw your Naira directly to your bank account." Every withdrawal it mentions is a naira withdrawal: "Keep Naira in your Quickchain wallet or withdraw instantly to any Nigerian bank account."
- **Custody.** The balance is a naira wallet held by the provider.
- **Sources we could not read.** The website [quickchain.app](https://www.quickchain.app/) is a JavaScript application that rendered no text for us, so we could not read its terms. The help-centre article we found returned 404. This analysis rests on the Google Play description.

Bitcoin sent to QuickChain is converted to a custodial naira balance on arrival, and the app describes no way to send BTC out. We therefore list it as [cannot send or receive bitcoins](/methodology/#nosendreceive).
