## App Description

This app is a Canadian-registered cryptocurrency trading platform operated by Newton Crypto Ltd., allowing users to buy, sell, and hold supported digital assets via a custodial account.

The app supports Bitcoin, along with dozens of other cryptocurrencies.

## Analysis

To quote from their FAQ: 

> we store the vast majority of our crypto in secure locations with no access to the internet. We also back up our keys in a distributed manner, meaning there are multiple redundancies in place to prevent accidental loss.
>
> Newton has partnered with Coinbase to provide institutional-grade storage for our customers' digital assets at no cost.

**This is a custodial service**.
