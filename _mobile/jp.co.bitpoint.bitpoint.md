## App Description

BITPOINT is a Japanese crypto exchange brand. Its app is now published by SBI VC Trade Co., Ltd., which merged with the former operator, BITPOINT Japan, on 1 April 2026. The app trades about twenty crypto assets including Bitcoin and offers staking and crypto lending. The Play Store description calls it a "Trading app for customers with a BITPOINT account" and warns: "In the event of our bankruptcy, we may not be able to return deposited funds and crypto assets." The same company publishes the [SBI VC Trade app](/mobile/co.jp.sbivc.trade.app/), and BITPOINT accounts are scheduled to move there.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play Store description lists "Bitcoin (BTC)" first among the "Supported Cryptocurrencies".
- **Sending and receiving.** BITPOINT's help article on crypto deposits ([暗号資産の入金方法を教えてください。](https://support.bitpoint.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/4888071228830)) tells users to open "入出金" (Deposits/Withdrawals), press "入金" (Deposit) for the asset, and send to the displayed deposit address from "送金元の取引所やウォレット" ("the sending exchange or wallet"). Another article ([プライベートウォレットからの入金・出金はできますか](https://support.bitpoint.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/53764918559897)) says: "プライベートウォレットから BITPOINTサービス への入金、および BITPOINTサービス からプライベートウォレットへの出金はいずれも可能です" ("both deposits from a private wallet to the BITPOINT service and withdrawals from the BITPOINT service to a private wallet are possible"). Help articles say that only assets on the "入金対象暗号資産" and "出金対象暗号資産" lists can be deposited or withdrawn ([deposit](https://support.bitpoint.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/53764879354777), [withdrawal](https://support.bitpoint.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/53764736129177)). Those lists are on www.bitpoint.co.jp/service/transfer/, which refused our requests with "このサイトはご利用の地域からはアクセスできません" ("this site cannot be accessed from your region"). The Play Store description does include BTC: it promises users avoid "a cryptocurrency exchange that charged fees for Bitcoin deposits and withdrawals".
- **Custody.** The Play Store description speaks of "deposited funds and crypto assets" that the company may be unable to return if it goes bankrupt. BITPOINT accounts are also being moved by the company: the help article on the service integration ([サービス統合および口座移管とはどのような内容ですか](https://support.bitpoint.co.jp/hc/ja/articles/56537230439321)) says "2026年12月末を目途にVCTRADEサービスへ口座および資産の移管を予定しております" ("we plan to transfer accounts and assets to the VCTRADE service around the end of December 2026"). A provider can only move customers' assets between its services if it holds them.

BITPOINT holds customers' crypto as deposits it can transfer between its own services, so the app is [custodial](/methodology/#custodial). Because BITPOINT accounts are due to migrate to VCTRADE by the end of 2026, this listing may soon describe a discontinued app.

## iOS

There is also an [iOS version on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1517332591). It is published by SBI VC Trade Co., Ltd., the same provider, and it gives access to the same account and service, so the same verdict applies to it.
