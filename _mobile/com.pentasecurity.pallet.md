**Update 2026-10-30:** This app was the Pallet wallet by AMO Labs, a subsidiary of Penta Security. Its [launch announcement from June 2018](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/with-new-wallet-app-pallet-amo-labs-takes-cryptocurrency-transactions-mainstream-300658180.html) describes a multicurrency wallet that supports Bitcoin, Ethereum and the AMO token, generates its keys inside the phone's Trusted Execution Environment, and follows BIP-32, BIP-39 and BIP-44. That makes it a self-custodial Bitcoin wallet. It also worked with Penta Security's hardware wallets over Bluetooth and NFC, see {% include walletLink.html wallet='hardware/pentasecurity.walletcard' verdict='true' %}. We found no source code for the app. It is still gone from Google Play and we found no archived copy of its store page. [Penta Security](https://www.pentasecurity.com) and [AMO Labs](https://www.amo.foundation) are both still in business. We now give the last released app the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosource" type='short' %}.

**Update 2022-03-06**: This app is not available anymore.
