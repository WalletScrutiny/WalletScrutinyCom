**⚠️ Warning:** Youbit went bankrupt in 2017. Its website is gone.

**Update 2026-10-30:** We now mark this app defunct and custodial. It is still gone from Google Play, and youbit.co.kr no longer resolves. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230813075721/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ddengle.bts) shows the app of the Youbit exchange, last updated in December 2017, with buy and sell trading and Bitcoin sending. Youbit stopped trading and filed for bankruptcy after its second hack in December 2017 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/12/19/yapian-youbit-exchange-files-bankruptcy.html), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2017/12/19/bitcoin-exchange-youbit-to-declare-bankruptcy-after-hack)). The hack took about 17 percent of the assets the exchange held in its wallets ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2017/12/19/south-korean-bitcoin-exchange-youbit-files-bankruptcy-following-successful-hack/)), and customers were told they would get only 75 percent of their holdings for now. Youbit held its users' coins, so the verdict is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

This is YouBit.co.kr's app for its exchange. The exchange has been [hacked in 2017](https://www.reuters.com/article/idUSKBN1ED0NJ) and subsequently declared bankruptcy.
