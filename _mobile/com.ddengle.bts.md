**⚠️ Warning:** Youbit went bankrupt in 2017. Its website is gone.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The provider is gone. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20230813075721/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ddengle.bts) shows the app of the Youbit exchange, with buy and sell trading and Bitcoin sending; Youbit stopped trading and filed for bankruptcy after its second hack in December 2017 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/12/19/yapian-youbit-exchange-files-bankruptcy.html), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2017/12/19/bitcoin-exchange-youbit-to-declare-bankruptcy-after-hack)). Youbit held its users' coins, so we change the verdict from {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %} to {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

This is YouBit.co.kr's app for its exchange. The exchange has been [hacked in 2017](https://www.reuters.com/article/idUSKBN1ED0NJ) and subsequently declared bankruptcy.
