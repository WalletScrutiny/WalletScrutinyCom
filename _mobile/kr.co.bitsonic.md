**⚠️ Warning:** The Bitsonic exchange is gone. Users had filed a criminal complaint against its CEO over unpaid withdrawals ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://www.sedaily.com/article/13143852)), and bitsonic.co.kr now hosts an unrelated guide to other exchanges.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play and the exchange is gone. On July 30, 2021 Bitsonic announced a suspension "for renewal" until November 30, 2021, two months after 39 users had accused its CEO of fraud and embezzlement over about 6.16 billion won they could not withdraw; the industry saw it as the start of a closure ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://www.sedaily.com/article/13143852)). By [December 2023](http://web.archive.org/web/20231208213325/http://www.bitsonic.co.kr/) the domain redirected to an ad page, and today it hosts an unrelated exchange troubleshooting guide. The [archived Play listing](http://web.archive.org/web/20221023220930/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.co.bitsonic) describes an exchange with its own BSC token, staking and "KRW and Crypto currency withdrawals" restricted by verification level, so the provider held users' coins. The verdict is now {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, and the listing is marked defunct.

## Verdict

This app is currently under maintenance, as seen in the website and the app. Until the app's scheduled release date, November 11, 2021, this app is **defunct.**
