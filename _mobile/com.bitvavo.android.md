## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** The exchange account is still custodial. Bitvavo's help
center says it
[manages your private keys for you](https://support.bitvavo.com/hc/en-us/articles/46832933766417-What-is-the-Web3-Wallet-and-how-does-it-work).
The app now also offers an optional non-custodial "Web3 wallet" with exportable
private keys, but it
[generates only EVM and Solana addresses](https://support.bitvavo.com/hc/en-us/articles/46668051223441-How-do-private-keys-and-passkeys-work),
so it does not hold Bitcoin. The two support links below are dead; the current
[private keys article](https://support.bitvavo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405186019729-Where-can-I-find-the-private-keys-of-my-hosted-wallet)
lives in the new help center. The verdict
{% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %} holds.

> Buy Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other cryptocurrencies within minutes and store them in your integrated Bitvavo wallet.

The description says that it's a Digital Asset & Cryptocurrency Exchange. This marks it as possibly custodial.

You can deposit and withdraw, but QR codes aren't provided.

> [Before you can withdraw digital currencies](https://support.bitvavo.com/l/en/article/vp1kqa4i31-send-to-external-wallet#deadLink) to an external address, you first need to add it to your address book, and get it verified.

Bitvavo requires verification before you can withdraw to external addresses.

> [As Bitvavo offers hosted wallets](https://support.bitvavo.com/l/en/article/3csr6klkfp-private-keys-hosted-wallet#deadLink), it is unfortunately not possible to provide the private keys of individual wallets. 

This confirms it as **custodial** and thus **not verifiable.**
