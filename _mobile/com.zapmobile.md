## App Description

Zap is a crypto wallet and exchange for Nigeria, published by Zap Technology (Zap Technology Limited, Lagos). It offers buying and selling for NGN, withdrawals to Nigerian bank accounts, a multichain wallet covering "Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum" and more, sending by @username and a dApp browser. The Play Store description calls it "a self-custody crypto wallet and exchange" with "your own keys and no third party holding your funds", and the option to import a wallet with a seed phrase.

## Analysis

- **Same provider, already listed.** The website [zap.africa](https://zap.africa/) links to this app (`com.zapmobile`) on Google Play and to App Store app `id6474125933`. That App Store app is already on WalletScrutiny together with the provider's earlier Android app `com.zapwallet.app`, as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.zapwallet.app' verdict='true' %}.
- **Bitcoin support.** The description lists Bitcoin among the supported networks and offers "Instant NGN → BTC". The website's footer lists Bitcoin under "Supported Coins".
- **Earlier test of the provider's app.** Our review of `com.zapwallet.app` imported a 12-word seed phrase into the app. The Bitcoin addresses the app showed did not match any of the addresses that phrase should produce. A phrase generated by the app and imported into Electrum (BIP-39, `m/84h/0h/0h`) did not reproduce the app's addresses either. The user's backup therefore did not give them control of the app's Bitcoin addresses. We have not tested the current Android app.
- **The terms contradict "no one can freeze".** The website claims: "No one can censor you, freeze your assets or stop your transactions." The [Terms of Service](https://zap.africa/termsofservice) say otherwise. Clause 9.7 states: "Zap has the right to freeze the funds for the subsequent return of funds to the victims with the assistance of law enforcement agencies". Clause 14.2 says Zap "will not hesitate to seize, freeze, terminate Users’ accounts and funds which are flagged out or investigated by legal mandate." A provider that can freeze and seize user funds controls those funds.

The provider's own terms reserve the right to freeze and seize funds. Our earlier test found that the backup of the provider's app did not control its Bitcoin addresses. Nothing published since shows that this has changed, so we rate this app {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, like the provider's other listings.

**Custodial: The provider holds the keys**
