**⚠️ Warning:** Bittrex is shut down. Bittrex, Inc. closed its US exchange on 2023-04-30 and [filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy](https://decrypt.co/139368/american-crypto-exchange-bittrex-files-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy) on 2023-05-08. [Bittrex Global](https://bittrexglobal.com/) then wound down too: trading has ended and its Liechtenstein and Bermuda companies are in liquidation.

## Android

This app is an interface to a trading platform:

> The Bittrex Global mobile app allows you to take the premiere crypto trading
  platform with you wherever you go.

As such, it lets you access your account with them but not custody your own
coins and therefore is **not verifiable**.
