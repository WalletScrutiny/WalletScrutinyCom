## App Description

XOIBIT claims to be a global cryptocurrency exchange app offering spot trading for Bitcoin and over 200 digital assets, with live charts, advanced order types, and professional market tools. The app claims to provide an integrated wallet experience that allows users to buy, sell, send, and receive cryptocurrencies, alongside fiat on-ramps and API access for advanced trading. XOIBIT is operated by XOIBIT GLOBAL CORP, which claims to offer secure infrastructure, cold storage, and 24/7 customer support for users worldwide.

## Analysis

The exchange's use of cold storage is a good indicator that the private keys are held by them and not the user. Therefore, this is a **custodial** service.
