**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is gone from Google Play; the Internet Archive still saw its [store page in November 2020](https://web.archive.org/web/20201123163440/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.benoitbasset.liqapi). It was an unofficial client for the Liquid (Quoine) exchange to check balances and place orders, as the [developer's page](http://liqapi.oblade.net/) still says. Liquid, owned by FTX, [halted withdrawals and then all trading](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ftx-owned-liquid-exchange-pauses-all-trading-after-withdrawal-halt) in November 2022 under FTX's Chapter 11 case, and [liquid.com](https://www.liquid.com/) now only points customers to the FTX claims portal, so the app has nothing left to connect to. Neither page mentions deposits or withdrawals, and we cannot test a removed app, so it stays a work in progress.

**Update 2021-11-18**: This app is not on Play Store anymore.
