**⚠️ Warning:** The provider appears to be out of business. Its domain cryptorize.co expired in 2024 and no longer resolves.

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. An [archived Play page from January 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210118060547/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.panthelope.cryptorize.mobile.cryptorize) shows a Nigerian app by Panthelope Technologies to send and receive Bitcoin and other coins and cash out to Nigerian or Ghanaian bank accounts. It claims "You have full control of all your crypto" and "No Sign up", but also offers a "Stash" that earns interest, and it never says who holds the keys, so we keep it as {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %}. The website named on that page, cryptorize.co, showed a [registry "domain expired" notice in September 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240913203815/http://cryptorize.co/) and no longer resolves.

**Update 2022-01-15**: This app is not on the Store anymore.
