**⚠️ Warning:** FCoin is gone. The exchange stopped withdrawals and shut down in February 2020 owing its users thousands of bitcoins.

**Update 2026-10-30:** FCoin was a custodial exchange that collapsed in February 2020. Its founder admitted it could not pay out [7,000 to 13,000 BTC to its users](https://cointelegraph.com/news/chinese-exchange-fcoin-closes-down-still-owes-users-125-million), and an on-chain analysis suggested [customer bitcoins had been moved out since 2019](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2020/02/26/error-or-plunder-report-suggests-fcoin-purposely-moved-customer-bitcoin-since-2019). An [archived store page from November 2020](https://web.archive.org/web/20201127180128/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fcoin.exchange) describes it as a "digital assets trading platform" with BTC/USDT and other trading pairs. The app is still gone from Google Play and fcoin.com no longer resolves. We now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %} and mark it defunct.

**Update 2022-03-06**: This app is not available anymore.
