**Update 2026-10-30:** FCoin was a custodial exchange that collapsed in February 2020, unable to pay out [7,000 to 13,000 BTC to its users](https://cointelegraph.com/news/chinese-exchange-fcoin-closes-down-still-owes-users-125-million). Its [archived store page from November 2020](https://web.archive.org/web/20201127180128/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fcoin.exchange) describes a "digital assets trading platform", so we now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. The provider is gone.

**Update 2022-03-06**: This app is not available anymore.
