## App Description

Triv is an Indonesian crypto exchange operated by PT. Tiga Inti Utama of Surabaya, which the Play Store description says is "a licensed crypto exchange in Indonesia as per the license issued by The Commodity Futures Trading Supervisory Agency (BAPPEBTI)". The app offers spot trading in "1000+" assets, staking, and rupiah deposits and withdrawals to Indonesian banks. The description lists Bitcoin first among the assets users can "Buy and sell".

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play Store description says: "Buy and sell Bitcoin, Shiba Inu, BNB, Paypal, Perfect Money, USDT, Ethereum, US stocks and various other digital assets with Triv."
- **Deposits and withdrawals of BTC.** Triv's [terms of service](https://triv.co.id/en/home/term-of-service) describe both directions. For deposits, the user picks "Setor/Deposit pada aset kripto yang diinginkan" ("Deposit on the desired crypto asset"), copies the "alamat wallet" (wallet address), and "Deposit akan masuk ke akun Triv setelah transaksi pengiriman aset kripto dikonfirmasi pada jaringan blockchain" ("the deposit is credited to the Triv account once the crypto transfer is confirmed on the blockchain"). Under "Pengiriman Aset Digital ke Wallet Lain" ("Sending digital assets to another wallet"), the system "meneruskan proses pengiriman aset kripto kepada wallet tujuan melalui jaringan blockchain" ("forwards the crypto transfer to the destination wallet over the blockchain"). Triv's help articles on [receiving](https://triv.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/6000039132) and [sending](https://triv.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/6000039137) bitcoin say: "anda bisa menerima dan mengirim bitcoin ke semua pengguna bitcoin di seluruh dunia" ("you can receive and send bitcoin to all bitcoin users worldwide"), via "Deposit Coin" and "Send Coin". These articles are undated and describe a web dashboard, so they may be older than the current app.
- **Custody.** The terms define "Deposit" as "proses penyimpanan saldo Rupiah atau Aset Kripto oleh Pengguna" ("the process of storing a rupiah or crypto balance by the user") in the user's Triv account. They also state: "Triv berhak menutup Akun dan membekukan dana, Aset Kripto dan transaksi di dalamnya apabila menemukan aktivitas yang mencurigakan di dalam Akun tersebut hingga waktu yang tidak ditentukan" ("Triv has the right to close the account and freeze the funds, crypto assets and transactions in it if it finds suspicious activity, for an indefinite period"). Triv can only freeze crypto assets if it controls them.

Triv holds users' crypto in accounts it can freeze, so it is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
