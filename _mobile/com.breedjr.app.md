## App Description

Breedjr — Crypto to Naira Fast is published by Breedjr Technologies for users in Nigeria. It is an off-ramp: the user sends Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT or other supported coins to a deposit address shown in the app, and receives naira in a linked Nigerian bank account. The Play Store description says "Send your crypto and receive naira directly in your Nigerian bank account in under 2 minutes".

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The Play Store description lists "Supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT and more" and mentions "cash out Bitcoin". Bitcoin can be sent to the service, but only to be sold.
- **No balance.** Breedjr's guide [How to convert USDT to naira in Nigeria](https://breedjr.com/blog/how-to-convert-usdt-to-naira-in-nigeria) says: "Breedjr does not require you to keep crypto sitting in a wallet balance before withdrawing. Your crypto is converted and the naira equivalent is paid to your connected bank account." Its guide [How to receive USDT in Nigeria when a client pays you](https://breedjr.com/blog/how-to-receive-usdt-in-nigeria-when-a-client-pays-you) describes the deposit address as "an address that ends in naira": "When the USDT lands, it is converted and the naira goes to your bank account. There is no balance to sell afterwards". The same article tells readers: "If you want to hold USDT, use a wallet or exchange."
- **No crypto withdrawal.** We found no description of sending crypto out of Breedjr on its [website](https://breedjr.com), in these two guides or in the Play Store description. The only payout described is naira to a bank account.

By the provider's own account, coins are converted to naira on arrival and the user never holds a crypto balance in the app. A one-way cash-out service is [not a wallet](/methodology/#nowallet).
