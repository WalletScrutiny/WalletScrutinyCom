**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. We now mark it
{% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. Its
[archived store page from April 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210411125127/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lastbit.cards)
says the app lets anyone "buy, sell, pay or get paid directly in bitcoin or euros"
over the lightning network. Euro balances need an account with the provider, and
Lastbit split its app into this one and
{% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.lastbit.lite' verdict='true' %},
whose terms say the user has "no control of, access to, or the ability to use"
the private keys. lastbit.io now redirects to [Striga](https://striga.com/), which
[Lightspark acquired](https://www.lightspark.com/news/lightspark/lightspark-acquires-striga)
in October 2025.

**Update 2021-09-09**: This app is not on Google Play anymore.
