**Update 2026-10-30:** We now mark this app {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. Its [archived store page from April 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210411125127/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lastbit.cards) says it lets anyone "buy, sell, pay or get paid directly in bitcoin or euros", and the terms of its sibling {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.lastbit.lite' verdict='true' %} say the user has "no control of, access to, or the ability to use" the private keys. lastbit.io now redirects to [Striga](https://striga.com/), which [Lightspark acquired](https://www.lightspark.com/news/lightspark/lightspark-acquires-striga) in October 2025.

**Update 2021-09-09**: This app is not on Google Play anymore.
