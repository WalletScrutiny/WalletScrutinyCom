## App Description

This is the "X-Meta Global" app of X-Meta LLC, a crypto exchange in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, that says it is "powered by Binance Cloud". It offers spot and futures trading, buying crypto with Mongolian tögrög (MNT) by bank transfer or with a card through Mercuryo, staking and a trading bot. It replaces {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.cloud.xmeta' verdict='true' %}. X-Meta's [website](https://www.x-meta.com/en) links to this app on both stores and shows a VASP "License" link to Mongolia's Financial Regulatory Commission.

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** The website has a [BTC/USDT spot market](https://www.x-meta.com/en/spot/BTCUSDT), and the Play description says users can "buy and sell cryptocurrency like BTC and IHC".
- **Sending and receiving.** The [Terms of Use](https://www.x-meta.com/en/category/terms-of-use) say: "Upon successful registration, the Customer may trade and deposit Virtual Assets into the Platform" and "The Customer may submit requests to withdraw Virtual Assets in accordance with these Terms of Use." X-Meta's old support center has a guide, [How to withdraw cryptocurrency](https://web.archive.org/web/20251009074311/https://support.x-meta.com/hc/en-us/articles/4744790014361-How-to-withdraw-cryptocurrency-X-META-Application) (archived October 2025), where users "Enter the recipient address" and are told "the exchange's 24-hour spending limit is 0.2BTC". That support site no longer resolves.
- **Custody.** The Terms of Use define a "Supported Virtual Asset" as one "approved by X-Meta for trading, transferring, and custody on the Platform". One of X-Meta's obligations is "To provide Virtual Asset trading, custody, and transfer services". X-Meta may "Freeze or restrict your Account" and "Suspend deposits or withdrawals of Virtual Assets or Fiat Currency". For forks and airdrops, it says "the Customer may be required to transfer the relevant Virtual Assets to a personal wallet". So coins on X-Meta are not in a wallet the user controls.

X-Meta holds the customers' crypto, so the app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}, like the app it replaces.
