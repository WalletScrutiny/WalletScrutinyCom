## App Description

Lemon is a Latin American crypto wallet and exchange app to buy, send, receive, and trade cryptocurrencies across 19 blockchain networks. It supports Bitcoin (BTC) and more than 30 other tokens, enabling direct on-ramp purchases in Argentina and P2P peso-crypto exchanges. The app includes Lemon Earn for staking-style yields and a Proof of Reserves section for solvency verification.
