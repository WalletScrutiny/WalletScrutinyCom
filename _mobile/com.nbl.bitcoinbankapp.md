**Update 2026-10-30:** This app is still gone from Google Play. An [archived copy of its store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210617191652/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nbl.bitcoinbankapp) from June 2021 names the developer Nebel Infotech and describes a "trading robot" that "automatically bets on the price of Bitcoin on behalf of users" and promises "up to 400% in daily profits". The page names no website and does not say whether the app itself holds any coins or only signs users up with a broker, so it stays a work in progress.

**Update 2021-08-12**: This app is not on the Play Store anymore
