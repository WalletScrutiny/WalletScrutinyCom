## App Description

Freedx is a cryptocurrency trading application that, according to the developer, operates as a licensed digital asset exchange rather than a standalone wallet. The developer states that Freedx allows users to buy, sell, and manage cryptocurrencies through spot trading, supporting assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Dogecoin, and Cardano. The service is presented as a regulated Bitcoin and Digital Asset Service Provider in El Salvador, with features including real-time trading, portfolio access via mobile and web, and integrated custodial wallet functionality.

## Analysis

The developer also advertises security measures such as cold storage, encryption, and two-factor authentication, alongside educational resources and customer support for traders.

The use of cold storage makes this a **custodial** service.
