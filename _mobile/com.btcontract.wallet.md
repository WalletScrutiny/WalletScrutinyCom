One of the earliest Bitcoin Lightning wallets on Google Play, SBW launched in 2015 and amassed over 100,000 downloads. Its build was consistently reproducible across multiple verified versions. In November 2024, however, the app quietly disappeared from the Play Store — though it remains accessible on F-Droid.

More concerning is what happened to its codebase. As of December 2025, the original [repository](https://github.com/btcontract/wallet) no longer points to the app's source — it now redirects to an entirely unrelated [project](https://github.com/Tactical-Advantage-Trading/wallet), suggesting the GitHub account itself may have changed hands.

The lineage of this app's digital assets is now unclear. We strongly advise against installing it.
