**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is gone from Google Play; the Internet Archive still saw its [store page in February 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260210040434/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.benoitbasset.hitapi). It was an unofficial client for the HitBTC exchange to check balances and place orders, as the [developer's page](http://hitapi.oblade.net/) still says. Neither page mentions deposits or withdrawals, and we cannot test a removed app, so it stays a work in progress.
