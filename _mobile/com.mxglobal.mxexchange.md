## App Description

MX is the app of MX Global, a Malaysian exchange registered with the Securities Commission Malaysia. It trades Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana and Worldcoin against the Malaysian ringgit. The Play description says "All crypto and fiat deposits are held via independent custody providers - we never touch this", under the heading "YOUR KEYS ARE SAFE HERE".

## Analysis

- **Bitcoin support.** MX Global's [Terms & Conditions](https://mxglobal.com.my/en/legal/) describe a hosted "MX Wallet" for "Supported Digital Asset, currently BTC, ETH or XRP only". The [fees page](https://mxglobal.com.my/product/) lists a BTC deposit fee of 0% and a BTC withdrawal fee for "Send to BTC Address" that is "Dynamic". The [FAQ](https://mxglobal.com.my/en/faq) says "you definitely can transfer out digital asset from your MX wallet to other wallets by entering your receiver wallet address". It adds that users "send and receive Digital Assets ... via the apps". Fiat withdrawals are MYR only, to a Malaysian bank account in the user's own name.
- **Custody.** Section 14.2 "PRIVATE KEYS" of the [Terms & Conditions](https://mxglobal.com.my/en/legal/) states: "MX Global along with the Business Wallet service provider of the Exchange and the appointed independent trustee securely stores all Supported Digital Asset private keys". It adds: "MX Global shall retain full ownership and control of the Private Keys associated with your MX Account and that you shall have no control of, access to, or the ability to use, such Private Keys".
- **Discrepancy.** The store description's "YOUR KEYS ARE SAFE HERE" and "we never touch this" do not match the terms, which give MX Global "full ownership and control of the Private Keys".
- **Name.** We found no connection between MX Global and MEXC, which is listed separately as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.mexcpro.client' verdict='true' %}.

By MX Global's own terms, the user has no access to the keys, so the app is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.
