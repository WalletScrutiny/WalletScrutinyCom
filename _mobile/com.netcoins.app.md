## App Description

Netcoins is a custodial cryptocurrency exchange operated by Netcoins USA Inc., a subsidiary of publicly traded BIGG Digital Assets (TSX-V: BIGG, OTCQX: BBKCF). The platform holds user private keys through third-party custodians BitGo (cold storage) and Fireblocks (hot wallet management), supporting over 30 cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin. Netcoins USA Inc. is a registered Money Service Business with FinCEN (MSB #31000278573839) and also operates in Canada under FINTRAC, CSA, and BCSC regulation.

## Analysis

From their [blog post](https://blog.netcoins.com/the-advantages-of-centralized-crypto-custody-with-netcoins/):

> As a centralized exchange, Netcoins takes on the responsibility of holding users private keys, meaning that when clients deposit cryptocurrencies into their Netcoins accounts, the exchange assumes control over those assets.

The platform uses third-party custodians (BitGo and Fireblocks) to manage user assets, making it a **custodial** service where users do not control their private keys.
