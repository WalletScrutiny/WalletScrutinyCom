**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its [archived store page](http://web.archive.org/web/20210505123414/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alpha.wallet) from May 2021, by Alpha Hold S.L. and written in Russian, described a Bitcoin, Ethereum and Litecoin wallet that stores funds on the device, available "only to you", with backup and recovery from 12 words. That makes it self-custodial. We found no source code for it on GitHub, and the developer's site alphahold.org no longer resolves, so it is {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosource" type='short' %}.

**Update 2021-10-07**: This app is not on Play Store anymore.
