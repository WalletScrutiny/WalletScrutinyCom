## Android

**Update 2026-10-30:** The app now advertises a "Web3 Wallet with more than 50
tokens supported on 15 Blockchains" in its store description, and YouHodler's
[Web3 wallet page](https://www.youhodler.com/wallet) says "Your private keys are
encrypted on your device only - we can't see or access them." That page lists
Bitcoin among its coins, but the [Bitcoin page](https://www.youhodler.com/wallet/btc)
only talks about "enterprise-level encryption" and says nothing about who holds
the keys. We have not tested the wallet in the app. The main account is still
custodial: in October 2026 its Italian subsidiary
[received MiCA authorization](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bingx-youhodler-esma-mica-register-friday-update)
for, among other services, custody. Until a test shows a self-custodial Bitcoin
wallet in the app, the verdict stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}.

This app is the interface to an exchange and might have a non-custodial part to
it but if so, it is not well advertised on their website and we assume it is
custodial and therefore **not verifiable**.
