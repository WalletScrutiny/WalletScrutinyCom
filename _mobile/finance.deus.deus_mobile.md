**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play. Its [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210526124732/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=finance.deus.deus_mobile) describes a front end for the DEUS DeFi protocol's synthetic-asset trading, and does not say whether the app held keys itself or connected to an outside wallet. The [DEUS Finance website](https://www.deus.finance/) is still online. The protocol was exploited several times, most recently in [May 2023](https://www.halborn.com/blog/post/explained-the-deus-dao-hack-may-2023) for about $6.5 million. Without evidence on key handling, it stays {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="wip" type='short' %}.

**Update 2022-02-25**: This app is not available anymore.
