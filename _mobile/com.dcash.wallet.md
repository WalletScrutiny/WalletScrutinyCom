**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play, the iPhone app it pointed to is gone from the App Store, and [renrenbit.com](https://renrenbit.com) now shows only a "Coming Soon" placeholder. RenrenBit's founder Zhao Dong was [reported detained by Chinese police in July 2020](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitfinex-investor-reportedly-detained-by-the-chinese-authorities) and, according to China's Supreme Procuratorate, [sentenced to seven years in prison](https://www.ccn.com/news/china-crypto-zhao-dongs-prison-sentence/) for illegal business operations and illegal foreign exchange trading. We found no archived copy of the Play Store page, so the verdict stays `wip`.

This app looks like a custodial offering:

> RenrenBit is a digital asset management platform offering 100% Reserve.

As it only has 100 downloads its category is "few users" though.
