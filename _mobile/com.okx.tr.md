## App Description

OKX TR is a cryptocurrency exchange operated by OKX for the Turkish market, offering trading in Bitcoin and other digital assets with Turkish Lira (TRY) fiat on-ramp integration through local banking partners including Fibabanka, VakıfBank, Ziraat Bankası, İş Bankası, Şekerbank, and Türkiye Finans. 

The platform supports spot trading, staking, and includes access to Web3 features, with the ability to withdraw cryptocurrencies to external wallets. Per OKX's terms of service for the centralized exchange, "Fiat Currencies and/or Digital Assets may be held by a third party appointed in good faith by OKX, or by OKX's nominees or sub-custodians", indicating custodial control of user assets. 

OKX TR is an [official regional service launched by OKX](https://www.okx.com/learn/okx-officially-launches-okx-tr) on February 27, 2024, operating on the official tr.okx.com subdomain as part of OKX's global expansion.

## Analysis

The [terms state](https://tr.okx.com/en/help/user-agreement#11-custody-of-customer-crypto-assets):

> 11.2.1 Customer crypto asset balances shall be monitored on an institutional ledger system on a per-customer basis, segregated from the Company’s own accounts. The Customer acknowledges that, in accordance with the limits specified in the Working Procedures Communiqué, their crypto assets may be collectively held in one or more wallets by the Company.

This is a **custodial** service.
