**Update 2026-10-30:** AEX is gone. The exchange [limited withdrawals after a bank run](https://www.web3isgoinggreat.com/single/aex-crypto-exchange-limits-withdrawals-after-a-bank-run) in June 2022 and [suspended its services for a police investigation](https://www.web3isgoinggreat.com/?id=police-shut-down-the-aex-crypto-exchange) on 2022-07-17. Its site aex.com now shows a domain parking page. The iPhone app, last updated in September 2022, is still in the App Store, so we added it to this listing as defunct.
