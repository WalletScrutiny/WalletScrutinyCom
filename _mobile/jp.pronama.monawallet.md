**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from the App Store (the lookup for its ID returns nothing in the US and Japanese stores). [monawallet.net](https://monawallet.net/) is still online and describes MonaWallet as a simple Monacoin wallet whose recovery phrase is created on the phone, but its download button points to the removed App Store listing. The source is on GitHub as [monawallet/monawallet-ios](https://github.com/monawallet/monawallet-ios), a fork of the Bread wallet. We cannot test a removed app, so it stays a work in progress.
