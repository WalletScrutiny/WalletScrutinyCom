**Update 2026-10-30:** The app is still gone from Google Play, but the Internet Archive kept its [store page from July 2019](https://web.archive.org/web/20190719145048/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gobaba.cryptopos). It was a point-of-sale app for merchants: you logged in with a Gobaba merchant account, entered an amount, and Gobaba would "convert it to BTC and provide a wallet address". Gobaba was a [buy and sell exchange](https://web.archive.org/web/20190501173724/https://www.gobaba.com/), so payments went to addresses it controlled, which makes the app {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="custodial" type='short' %}. By February 2025 the [archived gobaba.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20250223112240/https://gobaba.com/) only said "Gobaba is for sale".

**Update 2021-11-26**: This app is not available anymore.
