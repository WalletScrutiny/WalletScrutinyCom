## App Description

Promoted as a multi-asset cryptocurrency wallet with features for storing, sending, and managing digital assets. The official website by SkyCrypto highlights ease of use and security but does not provide clear technical details about how keys are generated or controlled by the user.

## Analysis

We tested the app and posted the video on [X.com](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2051509487056634336). The app uses an email-based hosted account and includes account verification, trading, buying and selling, and P2P features.

During testing, we did not find seed phrases, private key backup, or another recovery method that would give the user full control over the wallet keys. The app behaves like a hosted crypto account rather than a self-custodial wallet.

This app is most likely a custodial service, and it is **not verifiable as a self-custodial Bitcoin wallet**.
