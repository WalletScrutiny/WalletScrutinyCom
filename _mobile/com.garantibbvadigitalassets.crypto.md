## Android

## App Description

Garanti BBVA Kripto is a mobile crypto platform that allows Garanti BBVA customers to store, trade, and transfer digital assets such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Ripple, Avalanche, and USD Coin.

The app provides crypto buying and selling features, including limit orders, price alerts, favorites lists, and detailed coin information screens.

It emphasizes security through regular internal and external audits, penetration testing, and continuous monitoring of blockchain activity and fraud risks.

Garanti BBVA Kripto positions itself as a bank-backed digital asset service designed to simplify cryptocurrency management and trading within the Garanti BBVA ecosystem.

## Analysis

We took a look at their website, and came across this statement:

> Cold Wallet Infrastructure
> Thanks to the highly secure offline wallet infrastructure, you can protect your crypto assets against possible attacks and other risks.

Translated from Turkish via Google Translate browser plugin:

> Soğuk Cüzdan Altyapısı
> Yüksek güvenlik sağlayan çevrimdışı cüzdan altyapısı sayesinde kripto varlıklarınızı olası saldırılara ve diğer risklere karşı korumanız sağlanmaktadır.

This is a centralized Turkish exchange, and the use of cold storage implies that this is a **custodial** service.
