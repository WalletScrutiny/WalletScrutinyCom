**⚠️ Warning:** Bitholic is gone. In August 2019 the exchange was renamed Bithumb Singapore under a brand licence from the Korean exchange Bithumb ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2019-08-08-crypto-exchange-bithumb-to-debut-in-singapore-via-local-platform-bitholic-35199)), and bitholic.com is now a page offering the domain for sale.


This app is an interface for an exchange. Your coins are stored on their
infrastructure. As a custodial service it is **not verifiable**.
