## App Description

All InX (com.exchange.allin) is a CeDeFi trading platform that integrates tokenized RWA markets, derivatives, and modular smart-asset management components.

The app supports Bitcoin, offering BTC deposits and withdrawals alongside multi-chain asset support such as ETH, BSC, TRON, and SOL.

Its architecture combines centralized infrastructure for account management with blockchain-based components such as audited smart contracts and RWA tokenization frameworks.

The platform’s “Tri-Core” system (RWA, derivatives, smart asset management) underpins a multi-product ecosystem designed for cross-asset trading and on-chain financial operations.

## Analysis

Registered successfully and [located the Bitcoin wallet](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/1989250752200777988), but no option offered access to the private keys.

The [terms](https://support.allinx.io/en/articles/11757472-all-inx-terms-of-use) bear signs that this is a **custodial** service:

> Restricting accounts, freezing assets, or liquidating open positions.
