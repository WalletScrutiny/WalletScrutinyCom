**Update 2026-10-30:** BitPay has [discontinued this app](https://support.bitpay.com/hc/en-us/articles/203058086-How-do-I-set-up-BitPay-Checkout-Point-Of-Sale-Application): "The BitPay Checkout app is no longer being developed or supported", and merchants are pointed to the browser-based "Quick Checkout for Web". An [archived copy of the store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210820233106/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitpay.checkout) shows what it was: a point-of-sale app for merchants to accept Bitcoin and Bitcoin Cash payments, with "automatic conversion" so that merchants get "paid in your local currency" to a connected bank account. The merchant could receive payments but never hold or send bitcoin from the app, so we now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosendreceive" type='short' %}. BitPay itself is still in business, and its wallet is listed as {% include walletLink.html wallet='android/com.bitpay.wallet' verdict='true' %}.

**Update 2021-09-28**: This app is not on the Store anymore.
