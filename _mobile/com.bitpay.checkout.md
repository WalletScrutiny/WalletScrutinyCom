**Update 2026-10-30:** BitPay has [discontinued this app](https://support.bitpay.com/hc/en-us/articles/203058086-How-do-I-set-up-BitPay-Checkout-Point-Of-Sale-Application): "The BitPay Checkout app is no longer being developed or supported". The [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210820233106/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitpay.checkout) shows a point-of-sale app where merchants received Bitcoin payments "paid in your local currency" to a bank account, so they never held or sent bitcoin from the app and we now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nosendreceive" type='short' %}.

**Update 2021-09-28**: This app is not on the Store anymore.
