**Update 2026-10-30:** BitPay has [discontinued this app](https://support.bitpay.com/hc/en-us/articles/203058086-How-do-I-set-up-BitPay-Checkout-Point-Of-Sale-Application): "The BitPay Checkout app is no longer being developed or supported". The [archived store page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210820233106/https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bitpay.checkout) shows a point-of-sale app where merchants received Bitcoin payments "paid in your local currency" to a bank account, so the app never held a balance and we now give it the verdict {% include verdictBadge.html verdict="nowallet" type='short' %}. The iPhone app, last updated in August 2020, is still in the App Store, so we added it to this listing as defunct with the same verdict.

**Update 2021-09-28**: This app is not on the Store anymore.
