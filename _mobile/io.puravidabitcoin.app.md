## Android

## App Description

Pura Vida Bitcoin is a Bitcoin and Lightning wallet for Costa Rica. The official website describes it as a wallet for receiving Bitcoin, making Lightning payments, merchant services, OTC services, and Bitcoin ATMs.

## Analysis

The Google Play listing describes the app as:

> An easy to use Custodial Bitcoin and Lightning wallet geared for Costa Rica.

The official wallet page says users register with a phone number:

> Your phone number will act as your Pura Vida account access, which allows you to easily recover your account if you replace your phone.

This is a clear custody signal. Account recovery through a phone number means the wallet is not controlled only by a seed phrase or private keys held by the user.

The iPhone App Store listing also says the wallet is based on Banking-as-a-Service infrastructure from Galoy. This supports the same conclusion: the app is a hosted Bitcoin and Lightning wallet service.

This app supports Bitcoin and Lightning, but it is explicitly described as **custodial**.
