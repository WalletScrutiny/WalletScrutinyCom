## App Description

Stack (formerly Lootbase) is a mobile front end for trading spot assets and perpetual futures on Hyperliquid, published by Lootbase. Users fund it by card, Google Pay or crypto (SOL, BTC, ETH). Per the Play Store description, "Cash balances are held in USDC". Users can "Withdraw BTC, SOL, ETH, or export your keys and manage your assets anytime on Hyperliquid". The help centre says that at sign-up the app creates embedded wallets through the MPC provider Privy, and that fiat payments go through MoonPay.

## Analysis

- **Where the balance lives.** The help article [How do I export my keys?](https://intercom.help/lootbase/en/articles/11380874-how-do-i-export-my-keys) says under "Where are my assets?": "You can view and manage all your positions directly on HL.xyz." The account is a Hyperliquid account, and the cash balance is USDC.
- **Bitcoin is a funding route.** According to [How do I withdraw?](https://intercom.help/lootbase/en/articles/11650632-how-do-i-withdraw), withdrawing BTC means you "Buy ETH, SOL, or BTC on Lootbase using your cash" and then "Send to your external ETH, SOL, or BTC address". It also says "Hyperliquid charges a one-time $1 fee for first-time sends". Hyperliquid itself has no native bitcoin. On Hyperliquid, BTC exists as a token backed by a bridge. For example, Unit's [deposit guide](https://docs.hyperunit.xyz/how-to/deposit) says the Bitcoin deposit address "was securely generated by Unit's MPC Guardian Network" and is "permanently tied to your address on Hyperliquid". Stack's help pages do not say which bridge it uses.
- **No Bitcoin wallet is described.** [Why can't I deposit?](https://intercom.help/lootbase/en/articles/10518071-why-can-t-i-deposit) says: "Currently, Lootbase supports deposits on select blockchain networks (e.g., Solana)". None of the help pages we read mention a Bitcoin wallet of the app's own. While it is in the app, "BTC" is a bridged token on Hyperliquid, not BTC on the Bitcoin network.
- **Custody (for completeness).** [How are my funds stored?](https://intercom.help/lootbase/en/articles/10518034-how-are-my-funds-stored) calls the wallets "self-custodial" and says that "only you can authorize transactions with your passkey". It also notes: "Our partner, MoonPay Inc. handles custody of funds and crypto during deposits and withdrawals." We did not look into Privy's key handling further, because the Bitcoin question already decides the verdict.

BTC held in Stack is a token on Hyperliquid. On the way in and out it is converted by a bridge or by MoonPay. Wrapped or bridged BTC on another chain is not bitcoin, so Stack is [a wallet, but not for Bitcoin](/methodology/#nobtc).
