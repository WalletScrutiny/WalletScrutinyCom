## Android

## App Description

Vaultex is a multi-chain cryptocurrency wallet that, according to the developer, allows users to manage, buy, and trade digital assets from a single mobile application. The developer claims the app supports Bitcoin alongside Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, Solana, and other blockchain networks. Vaultex is presented as a self-custody wallet with features that include on-chain transactions, portfolio tracking, token swaps, staking, NFT management, and dApp connectivity. The developer also advertises optional fiat purchases via debit cards, privacy-focused design choices, and additional services such as risk alerts and crypto-backed payment cards.

## Testing and Analysis

Their homepage is currently 502. We were able to [install the app and test it](https://x.com/BitcoinWalletz/status/2011963636429046012). However, its performance is marred by constant app failures.

We were able to find a Bitcoin wallet, but not the seed phrases. We were also not able to find any option that would allow to show it. 

Despite its claims, this app is **custodial**.
