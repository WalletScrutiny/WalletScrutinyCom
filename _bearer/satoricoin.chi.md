## Background

Satori Coin Chi is the entry model of the relaunched Satori Coin line by Satori Club Pte Ltd in
Singapore, in the spirit of the 2016
{% include walletLink.html wallet='bearer/satoricoin.original' verdict='true' %}. It went on sale in
[February 2026](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/satori-coin-enters-u-s-market) and holds 0.001 BTC.
It comes in two versions: Chi in silver-plated brass (mintage 9,999) and Chi Silver in 1 oz of 999
fine silver (39.95 mm, 32.89 g, mintage 999). There is no NFC.

## Analysis

According to the [product page](https://satoricoin.com/), the key structure is a

> Single key generated by us, under hologram.

The coin ships unloaded. At checkout the buyer pays into an escrow address and downloads a Loading
Kit. After delivery, the Loading Kit moves the funds from escrow to the coin's address. This protects
the buyer against theft in the mail, but it does not change who made the coin's key. To redeem, the
owner peels off the hologram and sweeps the QR code with any wallet.

The only private key of the coin is generated by the provider. Once the coin is loaded, Satori could
spend it if they kept a copy, and the buyer cannot tell. The coin is
{% include verdictBadge.html verdict="prefilled" type='short' %}.

The {% include walletLink.html wallet='bearer/satoricoin.gi' verdict='true' %} avoids this with a
2-of-2 multisig where the buyer generates the second key.

**Note:** The GitHub repository `Ihaytoo/satori` calls itself "The Official Repository for Satori
Coin", but it contains a claim portal for a Solana token called $SATORI. satoricoin.com does not link
to it. Satori Coin is sold for bitcoin only.
