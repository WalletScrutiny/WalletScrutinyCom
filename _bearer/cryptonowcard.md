## Product Description

Cryptonow is a Swiss-based provider offering physical cryptocurrency bearer cards that can be purchased at retail locations throughout Switzerland. The cards contain pre-generated private keys hidden under a scratch-off layer.

From their [How and where are the Cryptonow Wallets® manufactured?](https://www.cryptonow.ch/en-ch/secure-crypto-plattform):

> The Cryptonow Wallets® are manufactured in Switzerland under strict quality and security standards. Our wallets are produced using industrial printing systems that are PCI-DSS certified and encrypted. These printing processes adhere to the same standards used by banks and credit card manufacturers for their production.

## How It Works

The Cryptonow card is a **bearer instrument** containing:
- **Public address** (visible, for receiving funds and checking balance)
- **Private key in plain text** (hidden under scratch-off layer)

Users can:
1. **Purchase** at retail locations (no registration required)
2. **Hold sealed** as a bearer asset (transfer physically like cash)
3. **Redeem** by scratching off to reveal the private key
4. **Import** the private key into any compatible wallet

## Private Key Generation

> The Cryptonow card can be used as a **non-custodial bearer asset**. The private key is printed on the card under a scratch-off cover.

While Cryptonow claims the card is "non-custodial" once purchased, the fundamental issue remains: the provider generated the private keys, printed it at the back of the cards. Any step in the process would entail trusting the provider, its employees and instruments not to keep any copies.
